# 31976A0410(01)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

OPINION given by the Council pursuant to Article 50 (2) of the Treaty establishing the European Coal and Steel Community on a draft Commission Decision (ECSC) regarding the temporary postponement of the date of payment of the steel production levy

In a letter of 8 March 1976 the Commission sought the opinion of the Council pursuant to Article 50 (2) of the Treaty establishing the European Coal and Steel Community on a draft Commission Decision (ECSC) regarding the temporary postponement of the date of payment of the steel production levy.

At its 388th meeting, on 25 March 1976, the Council gave the opinion requested.
