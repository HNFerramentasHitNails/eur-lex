# Decisão da Comissão, de 11/12/2009 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.5690 - OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM CORPORATION / PHIBRO) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

|EUROPEAN COMMISSION |

Brussels, 11.12.2009

SG-Greffe(2009) D/ 11168

C(2009) 10248

PUBLIC VERSION

MERGER PROCEDURE ARTICLE 6(1)(b) DECISION

SIMPLIFIED PROCEDURE

To the notifying party:

Dear Madam(s) and/or Sir(s),

Subject: Case No COMP/M.5690 - OCCIDENTAL PETROLEUM CORPORATION/ PHIBRO Notification of 13/11/2009 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 [1] Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union No C 281 of 21/11/2009, page 54.

1. On 13/11/2009, the Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 by which Occidental Petroleum Corporation ("Occidental", USA) acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation control of the whole of Phibro LLC ("Phibro", USA) belonging to Citigroup by way of purchase of shares.

The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

- for Occidental: oil and gas exploration and production; manufacturing and sale of certain chemicals;

- for Phibro: trading in oil, gas, metals, commodities and commodities derivatives.

3. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 and of paragraph 5 (c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 [2] .

4. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.

For the Commission (signed) Philip LOWE Director General

[1] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1.

[2] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005, p. 32.
