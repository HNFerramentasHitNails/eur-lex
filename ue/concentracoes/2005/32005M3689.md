# Decisão da Comissão, de 31/01/2005 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.3689 - BT / ALBACOM) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

| | Brussels, 31.01.2005

SG-Greffe(2005) D/200430

To the notifying party

Dear Madam(s) and/or Sir(s),

Subject : Case No. COMP/M 3689 – BT/Albacom

Notification of 21/12/2004 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004[1] Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union No. C-4 of 7/1/2005, page 2

1. On 21/12/2004, the Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 by which the undertaking British Telecommunications plc (“BT”, United Kingdom) acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation control of the whole of the undertaking Albacom SpA (“Albacom”, Italy) by way of purchase of shares.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are :

- for BT: telecommunications services, internet products and IT solutions on a world-wide scale;

- for Albacom: telecommunications services and internet services to business customers in Italy.

3. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 and of paragraph 5, subparagraph (d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No139/2004[2].

4. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission

Neelie KROES Member of the Commission signed
