# Decisão da Comissão, de 05/12/2005 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.3997 - SUN CAPITAL / SARA LEE) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

To the notifying party

Dear Sir,

Subject : Case No. COMP/M.3997- SUN CAPITAL / SARA LEE

Notification of 26.10.2005 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004[1]

Publication in the Official Journal of the European Communities No. C 277, 10.11.2005, page 11.

1. On 26.10.2005, the Commission received a notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 by which the undertaking […] Sun Group (“Sun”, USA), acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation control of the European Branded Apparel division (“The Business”) of Sara Lee Corporation (USA), by way of purchase of shares.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

- for Sun : group of private equity investment funds;

- for The Business: design, manufacture, distribution and sale in Europe of branded apparel products, such as women’s intimates, men’s underwear, hosiery and socks.

3. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No.139/2004 and of paragraph 5, subparagraph b, of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004[2].

4. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission,

(Signed) Neelie KROES

Member of the Commission

[1] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004 p. 1.

[2] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005 p.32.
