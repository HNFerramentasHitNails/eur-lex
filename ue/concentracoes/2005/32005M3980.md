# Decisão da Comissão, de 18/11/2005 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.3980 - CANON / CANON ESPAс) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

| |Brussels, 18/11/2005

SG-Greffe(2005) D/206210

To the notifying party

Dear Madam(s) and/or Sir(s),

Subject : Case No. COMP/M.3980 - CANON / CANON ESPAÑA Notification of 11/10/2005 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [1] Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union No. C 261, 20/10/2005, page 9.

1. On 11/10/2005 , the Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 by which Canon Europa N.V. (“Canon”, The Netherlands) a wholly-owned subsidiary of Canon Inc. (Japan), will acquire sole control over Canon España S.A. (“Canon España”, Spain) by way of share purchase.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are as follows:

Canon: the manufacture and sale of office, consumer and industrial products.

Canon España: the wholesale distribution in Spain of Canon office and consumer products.

3. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 and of paragraph 5 subparagraph c, of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004[2].

4. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission

(signed) Neelie KROES Member of the Commission

[1] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004 p. 1

[2] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005 p.32
