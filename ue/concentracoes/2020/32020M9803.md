Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 22 June 2020, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which SAZKA Group
      a.s. (“SAZKA Group”, Czechia), controlled by KKCG AG, and ultimately controlled
      by the VALEA Foundation, acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the
      Merger Regulation sole control of the whole of OPAP (“OPAP”, Greece) by way of
      contract or any other means and by way of purchase of shares.3

2.    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

      −    for SAZKA Group: active in the lottery, gaming, and betting sectors in Czechia,
           Austria, and Italy;

      −    for OPAP: active in the lottery, gaming and betting sectors in Greece and Cyprus.

3.    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
      the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
      paragraph 5(b) and 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for
      treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4
