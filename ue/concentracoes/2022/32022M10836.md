Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 19 July 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which: the
      undertakings SSE Thermal Generation Holdings Limited (“SSE Thermal”) (United
      Kingdom), and Equinor New Energy Limited (“Equinor”) (United Kingdom), a
      subsidiary of Equinor ASA (Norway), will acquire within the meaning of Article
      3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of the undertaking
      Triton Power Holdings Limited (“Triton Power”) (Jersey), currently ultimately
      controlled by Energy Capital Partners (Unites Stated) by way of purchase of shares.3

2.    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

            United Kingdom and Ireland. SSE Thermal is part of the SSE Group, which
            develops, builds, operates and invests in low carbon electricity infrastructure. SSE

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
