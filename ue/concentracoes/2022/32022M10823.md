Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 19 July 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Clearlake
      Capital Group, L.P. (‘Clearlake’, U.S.A.), and TA Associates Management L.P. (‘TA
      Associates’, U.S.A.), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger
      Regulation joint control over the whole of Ephesoft, Inc. (‘Ephesoft’, U.S.A.) by way
      of purchase of shares.3

2.    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

           for Clearlake: private investment firm, active in software and technology-enabled
            services, energy and industrials, food and consumer products,

           for TA Associates: private equity firm, active in business services, consumer
            services, financial services, healthcare and technologies,

3.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
     paragraph 5(a) and (c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for
     treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
     compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
