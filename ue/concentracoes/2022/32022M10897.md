Dear Sir or Madam,

1.     On 3 November 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
       undertakings Prévoyance Dialogue du Crédit Agricole S.A.* (“Predica”, France),
       Vauban Infrastructure Partners S.C.A. (“Vauban”, France) and Telefonica S.A.
       (“Telefonica”, Spain) will acquire within the meaning of Articles 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of
       the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of the undertaking Bluevia Fibra,
       S.L. (“Bluevia”, Spain) by way of purchase of shares.3

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
