Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 4 August 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
      undertaking Knorr-Bremse AG (‘KB AG’, Germany) will acquire within the meaning
      of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control over Knorr-Bremse Systeme
      für Nutzfahrzeuge GmbH (‘KB SfN’, Germany), currently jointly controlled by KB
      AG and Robert Bosch GmbH (‘Bosch’, Germany), and Knorr-Bremse Commercial
      Vehicle Systems Japan Ltd. (‘KBJ’, Japan), currently jointly controlled by KB AG
      and Bosch, by way of purchase of shares.3

2.    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

          KB AG is a global supplier of braking and other systems for rail and commercial
           vehicles,

          KB SfN supplies pneumatic braking solutions for medium and heavy commercial
           vehicles, as well as other products including transmission management solutions
           (clutch actuation units and gear control units), dampers, and steering systems,

3.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
     paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of
     certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. 4

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
