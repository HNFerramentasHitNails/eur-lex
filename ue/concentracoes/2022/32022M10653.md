Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 19 May 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which the
      undertakings Estavayer Lait SA (‘Estavayer Lait’, Switzerland), part of Migros
      Group, Givaudan SA (‘Givaudan’, Switzerland) and Bühler AG (‘Bühler’,
      Switzerland), intend to acquire within the meaning of Articles 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the
      Merger Regulation joint control of a newly created joint venture company, Cultured
      Food Innovation Hub (Switzerland).3

2.   The concentration is accomplished by way of acquisition of shares in the newly
     created joint venture company.

3.   The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

     − Estavayer Lait is active in the production and packaging of dairy products, the
       production and ripening of cheeses, the production of cold sauces and the
       manufacturing of plant based dairy, cheese and meat alternative products,

     − Givaudan is a global manufacturer and supplier of fragrance and flavor products to
       the food, beverage and consumer goods industries. It also produces aroma
       chemicals that can be used as ingredients in the production of flavor or fragrance
       compounds,

     − Bühler is a globally active technology company which provides technological
       solutions for plants, equipment and related services for processing foods and
       manufacturing advanced materials. Its core activities are in technologies for
       transforming grain into flour and animal feeds, producing pasta and chocolate, and
       manufacturing die cast components.

4.   Cultured Food Innovation Hub will provide facilities and knowledge to accelerate the
     R&D work of third companies which are interested in becoming active in the area of
     cultured meat, cultured fish and seafood, as well as precision fermentation. Cultured
     meat, fish or seafood is a meat, fish or seafood produced by in vitro cell cultures of
     animal cells.

5.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
     paragraph 5(b) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of
     certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

6.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
     compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
     the EEA Agreement.
