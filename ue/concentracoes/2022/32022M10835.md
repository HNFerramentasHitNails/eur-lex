2.   The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

         Amber is an international sponsor and manager of infrastructure focused on long-
          term, lower risk, cash flow generative investments,

         Allianz Group is a global financial services group active in insurance and asset
          management,

         Dalmore is an independent fund manager focusing on lower risk opportunities for
          institutional investors in the UK infrastructure sector,

         Tideway is an entity granted a Licence under the Water Industry Act 1991 (of
          England and Wales) in 2015 to act as the infrastructure provider responsible for
          the design, construction, commissioning and maintenance of the Thames Tideway
          Tunnel, a 25km long tunnel running under London for the purposes of carrying
          sewerage, complemented by several connecting tunnels.

3.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
     paragraph 5(a) and (b) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for
     treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.5

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
     compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
     the EEA Agreement.
