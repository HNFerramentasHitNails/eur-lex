Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 12 July 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004, by
      which Madison Dearborn Partners, LLC (“MDP”, United States) and HPS Investment
      Partners, LLC (“HPS”, United States) will acquire within the meaning of Article
      3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of Premfina Limited
      (“Premfina”, United Kingdom), and i GO 4 Ltd. (“iGO4”, United Kingdom), by way
      of purchase of shares.3

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
