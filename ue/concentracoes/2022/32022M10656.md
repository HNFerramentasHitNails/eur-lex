Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 4 July 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which Flutter
      Entertainment plc (‘Flutter’, Ireland) will acquire within the meaning of Article
      3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation control of the whole of Sisal S.p.A. (‘Sisal’, Italy) by
      way of purchase of shares.3

2.    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

           Flutter: global sports betting, gaming and entertainment operator,

           Sisal: betting, gaming and lottery operator.
