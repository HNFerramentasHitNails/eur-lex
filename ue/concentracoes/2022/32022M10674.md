Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 11 April 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which                  the
      undertaking Interparking Servizi S.r.l. (‘Interparking’, Italy), ultimately controlled by
      the Ageas Group, and Ospedale San Raffaele S.r.l. (‘OSR’, Italy), ultimately
      controlled by Gruppo San Donato (‘GSD’), will acquire within the meaning of Article
      3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over three parking lots located
      in the San Raffaele area in Milan (the ‘Target’) by way of purchase of shares in a
      newly created company constituting a joint venture and by way of purchase of assets.3

2.   The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

          Interparking is active in the management of paid parking lots in Italy.
           Interparking is controlled by Ageas Group, which mainly provides life and non-
           life insurance services to personal, business and institutional customers, active in
           Europe and in Asia,

          OSR is a Scientific Hospitalization and Care Institute, part of GSD, a private
           hospital group in Italy,

          The Target consists of (i) an off-street parking lot and related assets located in
           Milan, in the area nearby the San Raffaele Hospital, currently owned by OSR
           and managed by a third party, as well as (ii) two parking lots (‘P6 and P7’)
           located in the same area, owned by Universita Vita-Salute San Raffaele and MM
           S.pA, and managed by OSR.


3.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
     paragraph 5(a) and 5(c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for
     treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
     compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
     the EEA Agreement.
