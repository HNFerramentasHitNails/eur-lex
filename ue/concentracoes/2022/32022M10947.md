Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 31 October 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
      undertakings Atlas Arteria, through Atlas Arteria Limited, (Australia) and Ontario
      Teachers’ Pension Plan (‘OTPP’, Canada), will acquire within the meaning of Article
      3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of Skyway Concession
      Company LLC (‘Skyway Concession Company’, USA), by way of purchase of
      shares.3
