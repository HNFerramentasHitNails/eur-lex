Dear Sir or Madam,

1.     On 22 November 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Montagu
       Private Equity LLP (‘Montagu’, United Kingdom), and Canada Pension Plan
       Investment Board (‘CPPIB’, Canada), will acquire within the meaning of Article
       3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of the
       Universal Investment GmbH (‘Universal Investment’, Germany), by way of purchase
       of shares in its parent company Alpha LuxCo 2 SARL (Luxembourg).3

            Northern Europe,
