Dear Sir or Madam,

1.     On 27 October 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
       undertaking PHO P GmbH, a subsidiary of the Schwarz Group (“PHO P”, Germany)
       will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole
       control over the whole of the undertaking Stora Enso Maxau GmbH (“Maxau”,
       Germany) by way of purchase of shares.3

2.     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

            PHO P is part of the Schwarz Group, active in food retailing, food products,
             and in the collection, sorting, processing, recycling and commercialization of
             waste,

            Maxau operates a paper manufacturing mill that produces supercalendered
             paper from recovered paper.

3.     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
