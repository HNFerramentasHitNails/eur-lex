Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 13 May 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which the
      undertakings Repsol Customer Centric, S.L. (“Repsol CC”, Spain), a subsidiary of
      Repsol, S.A (“Repsol”, Spain), and Telefónica de España, S.A.U. (“Telefónica”,
      Spain), a subsidiary of Telefónica S.A (“TEF”, Spain) will acquire within the meaning
      of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of a joint venture
      (the “Joint Venture”) by way of purchase of shares.3

2.    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

           Repsol: the commercialisation and transportation of oil and gas, the production
            and commercialisation of asphalt, fuels, specialised products, lubricants, and
            chemicals for different uses, as well as the development and management of
            service stations and the generation and commercialisation of electricity and gas,

     Spain mainly consisting in the acquisition, installation and maintenance of solar

     and related activities.

4.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
     paragraph 5(c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of
     certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

5.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
     compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
