      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which InfraVia V
      Invest S.à r.l. (Luxembourg), special purpose vehicle controlled by InfraVia Capital
      Partners SAS (InfraVia, France), and P4 sp. zo. o (P4, Poland), wholly controlled by

      currently wholly controlled by P4, by way of purchase of shares.3
