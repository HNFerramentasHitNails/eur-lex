1.    On 20 May 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed

      Partners SICAV-FIS S.A. ("CVC", Luxembourg) and Hartenberg Holding, s.r.o.
      ("HTB", Czech Republic) will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4)

      controlled by HTB by way of purchase of shares.3

           in Finland,

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
