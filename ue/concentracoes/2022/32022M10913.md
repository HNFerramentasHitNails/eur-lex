Dear Sir or Madam,

1.   On 17 November 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the undertakings
Saudi Aramco Development Co Ltd (“SADCO”, Saudi Arabia), controlled by Saudi
Arabian Oil Company (“Saudi Aramco”, Saudi Arabia), and Honeywell Automation and
Control Products Ltd (“HACP”, US), controlled by Honeywell International Inc.
(“Honeywell”, US), will acquire within the meaning of Articles 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the
Merger Regulation joint control of a newly created joint venture (“JV”, Saudi Arabia). The
concentration is accomplished by way of purchase of shares in the JV3.

     certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

5.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
