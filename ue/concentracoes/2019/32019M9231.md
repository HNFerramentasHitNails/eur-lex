              No 139/2004 and Article 57 of the Agreement on the European Economic Area


Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 20 December 2018, the European Commission received notification of a concentration
      pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the undertakings Etem Bulgaria,
      S.A. (“Etem”, Bulgaria) controlled by Viohalco, S.A., and Gestamp North Europe
      Services, S.L. (“Gestamp North Europe Services”, Spain) controlled by ACEK Desarrollo
      y Gestión Industrial, S.L. acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the
      Merger Regulation joint control over Gestamp Etem Automotive Bulgaria (“PostOps JVC
      Bulgaria”) and Etem Gestamp Aluminum Extrusions (“Production JVC” and together with
      PostOps JVC Bulgaria, the “JVs”), currently solely controlled by Etem.3

2.    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

          for Etem: its activities cover, inter alia, aluminium solutions and aluminium
           extrusions for various applications,

          for Gestamp North Europe Services: it engages in the design, development and
           manufacture of metal components and structural systems for the automotive industry,

          for Production JVC: it will manufacture aluminium extruded profiles,

          for PostOps JVC Bulgaria: it will comercialise and industrialise certain post-extrusion
           activities on extruded profiles.

3.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that the
     notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of paragraph 5(b) of
     the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations
     under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European Commission
     has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the
     internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of
     Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
