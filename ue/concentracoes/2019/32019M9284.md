              No 139/2004 and Article 57 of the Agreement on the European Economic Area


Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 5 April 2019, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which the undertakings
      BillerudKorsnäs Venture AB (“BillerudKorsnäs”, Sweden), controlled by Billerudkorsnäs
      AB, and ALPLA Holding GmbH (“Alpla”, Austria), belonging to the Alpla group, acquire
      within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and of Article 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint
      control over the whole of the undertaking ecoXpac A/S (“ecoXpac”, Denmark), currently
                                                                   3
      controlled by BillerudKorsnäs, by way of purchase of shares.

2.    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

      −    BillerudKorsnäs is a manufacturer of primary fibre-based packaging materials.

      −    ALPLA is a family operated developer and producer of plastic packaging solutions.

      −    ecoXpac is a research and development company developing technology for moulded
           pulp solutions for packaging purposes.

3.    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that the
      notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of paragraph 5(a) of
      the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations
      under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4
