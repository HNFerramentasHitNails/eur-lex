# Decisão da Comissão, de 25/08/2006 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.4343 - KRAFT FOODS / UNITED BISCUITS) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Brussels, 25-VIII-2006

SG-Greffe(2006)204783

To the notifying party

Dear Madam(s) and/or Sir(s),

Subject: COMP/M.4343 – Kraft Foods/United Biscuits

Notification of 25.07.2005 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [1];

Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union No. C 180 p. 98.

1. On 25/07/2006, the Commission received a notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 by which Kraft Foods International Inc. („Kraft Foods“, USA) acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation control of the whole of the undertaking United Biscuits Iberia S.L. („UB Iberia“, Spain) by way of purchase of shares.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

- Kraft Foods: Manufacture and sale of packaged food and beverages,

- UB Iberia: manufacture and sale of biscuits, desserts, fruit juice and other food stuff.

3. Due to board representation and veto rights, already prior to this transaction Kraft Foods has (negative) control over the United Biscuits group (and hence UB Iberia) [2]. With the notified transaction, Kraft Foods intends to acquire full sole control over UB Iberia and consequently, Kraft Foods will be able to unilaterally impose strategic decisions and to determine UB Iberia’s commercial policies. As a result, the notified transaction leads to a change in the quality of control of Kraft Foods and therefore constitutes a concentration in the meaning of Article 3 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004. [3]

4. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 and the scope of paragraph 5, subparagraph c of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [4].

5. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission

signed Philip Lowe

Director General

[1] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004 p. 1

[2] Case No. COMP/M.1920 Nabisco/United Biscuits; Case No. COMP/M.2072 Phillip Morris/Nabisco.

[3] Para. 40 of the Commission Notice on the concept of concentration.

[4] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005 p.32
