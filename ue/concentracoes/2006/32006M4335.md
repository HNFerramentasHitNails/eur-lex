# Decisão da Comissão, de 03/11/2006 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.4335 - DIEHL / THALES / JV) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Brussels, 03.11.2006

SG-Greffe(2006) D/206605

To the notifying parties

Dear Madam(s) and/or Sir(s),

Subject: Case No. COMP/M.4335 – Diehl / Thales / JV

Notification of 4.10.2006 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [1]

Publication in the Official Journal of the European Communities No. C 246, dated 13/10/2006, page 21.

1. On 4.10.2006, the Commission received a notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 by which the undertaking Thales S.A. (“Thales”, France) and Diehl Stiftung & Co. KG (“Diehl”, Germany) acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation joint control over part of the undertakings TDA Armements SAS (France), Thales Munitronic BV (The Netherlands), Forges de Zeebrugge (Belgium), currently controlled by Thales, and over the undertaking Junghans Feinwerktechnik GmbH & Co KG (Germany), currently controlled by Diehl, by way of contribution of assets in a newly created company constituting a joint venture.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

- for Thales: defence, aerospace and civilian security technology. For the assets conferred to the Joint Venture: production and sales of fuzes and safety and arming devices;

- for Diehl: metals, controls, and defence and aviation For the assets conferred to the Joint Venture: production and sales of fuzes and safety and arming devices.

3. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 and of paragraph 5, subparagraph b, of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 [2].

4. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission

signed Philip LOWE

Director General

[1] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004 p. 1.

[2] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005 p.32.
