# Decisão da Comissão, de 25/09/2006 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.4182 - HÖRMANN / MN / ACP JV) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Brussels, 25.09.2006

SG-Greffe(2006) D/205378

To the notifying parties

Dear Madam(s) and/or Sir(s),

Subject: Case No. COMP/M.4182 – HÖRMANN/MN/ACP JV

Notification of 21.08.2006 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [1]

Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union No. C 204, dated 26.08.2006, page 34

1. On 21.08.2006, the Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 by which the undertakings MAN Nutzfahrzeuge AG (“MN, Germany), belonging to the MAN-group (“MAN”, Germany) and Hörmann GmbH & Co. Beteiligungs KG (“Hörmann”, Germany) acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation joint control of the undertaking Automotive Components Penzberg GmbH (“ACP”, Germany), by way of purchase of shares.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are :

- for MN: design, manufacture and marketing of trucks, buses, components and related services;

- for MAN : commercial vehicles, diesel engines, turbo engines, industrial services, engineering services, construction engineering;

- for Hörmann: electrical, machine and construction engineering, radio engineering and related services;

- for ACP: production of metal structural components for heavy trucks.

3. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 and of paragraph 5 (b) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [2].

4. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission

signed Philip LOWE

Director General

[1] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004 p. 1

[2] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005 p.32
