# Decisão da Comissão, de 18/09/2006 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.4377 - DOUGHTY HANSON / CAUDWELL GROUP MOBILE DISTRIBUTION BUSINESS) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Brussels, 18.09.2006

SG-Greffe(2006) D/205213

To the notifying party:

Dear Madam(s) and/or Sir(s),

Subject: Case No. COMP/M.4377 - Doughty Hanson / Caudwell Group Mobile Distribution Business

Notification of 17/08/2006 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [1]

Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union No. C 209, 31/08/2006, page 11.

1. On 17/08/2006, the Commission received a notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 [2] by which the undertaking Doughty Hanson & Co. Limited (“DOUGHTY HANSON”, UK) acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation control of the whole of the Caudwell Group Mobile Distribution Business (“Mobile Distribution Business”, UK) by way of purchase of shares.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

- for the undertaking DOUGHTY HANSON: private equity fund manager

- for the Mobile Distribution Business: distribution and repair of mobile handsets and accessories, as well as ancillary services (e.g. customisation/kitting of mobile phones and packaging, sale of tariff-based contracts to consumers and businesses, sale of mobile phone top-up vouchers) and recruitment of professionals.

3. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 and of paragraph 5, subparagraph b, of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [3].

4, For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission

Signed Philip LOWE

Director General
