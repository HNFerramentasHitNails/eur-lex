# Decisão da Comissão, de 27/10/2006 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.4385 - HSBC / LAMDA / OLYMPIA VILLAGE) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Brussels, 27.10.2006

SG-Greffe(2006) D/206488

To the notifying parties

Dear Sirs,

Subject: Case No. COMP/M.4385 – HSBC/ Lamda/ Olympia Village

Notification of 22.09.2006 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [1]

Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union No. C234, 29.09.2006, p.8

1. On 22.09.2006, the Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 by which HSBC Property Investments Luxembourg S.a.r.l. (“HSBC PIL”, Luxemburg) belonging to the HSBC group and Lamda Development S.A. (“Lamda”, Greece) acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation joint control of The Mall, a real estate development held by Lamda Olympia Village S.A. (“Olympia Village, Greece), by way of purchase of shares.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are :

- for undertaking HSBC PIL : real estate investment and management;

- for Lamda: real estate development;

- for The Mall: the exploitation of a commercial retail property in Athens.

3. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 and of paragraph 5, subparagraph b, of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [2].

4. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission

signed Philip LOWE

Director General

[1] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004 p. 1

[2] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005 p.32
