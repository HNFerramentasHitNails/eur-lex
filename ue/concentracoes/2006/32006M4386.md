# Decisão da Comissão, de 06/10/2006 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.4386 - CINVEN / AERO INVEST) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Brussels, 06.10.2006

SG-Greffe (2206) D/205648

To the notifying party

Dear Madams and Sirs,

Subject: Case COMP/M.4386 - CINVEN LIMITED/AERO INVEST 1 S.P.A

Notification of 01.09.2006 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EEC) No. 139/2004 [1]

Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union No. C

1. On 1 september 2006, the Commission received a notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 Cinven Limited (“Cinven”, UK), controlled by Cinven Group Limited (“Cinven Group”, UK), acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation the sole control of Aero Invest 1 S.p.A. (“Aereo”, Italy) controlling Avio S.p.A., which in turn controls a group of companies, by way of purchase of shares.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

- for Cinven: private equity investment;

- for Aero: is the ultimate company of the Avio Group which controls a number of companies operating in the aerospace and defence industry.

3. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EEC) No. 139/2004 and of paragraph 5, subparagraph c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EEC) No 139/2004 [2].

4. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EEC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission,

signed Philip Lowe

Director General

[1] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004 p. 1

[2] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005 p.32
