# Decisão da Comissão, de 12/12/2006 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.4467 - BLACKSTONE / PAI / UNITED BISCUITS) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Brussels, 12-XII-2006

SG-Greffe(2006) D/207645

To the notifying parties

Dear Madam(s) and/or Sir(s),

Subject: Case No. COMP/M.4467 – Blackstone / PAI / United Biscuits : Notification of 07/11/2006 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [1]

Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union No. C 277 – 15/11/2006 – page 24.

1. On 07.11.2006, the Commission received a notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 [2] by which the undertakings The Blackstone Group (“Blackstone”, USA) and PAI Partners (“PAI”, France), through an acquisition vehicle, acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation indirect joint control of the undertaking United Biscuits (“United Biscuits”, UK) by way of purchase of shares.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are :

- for Blackstone : private merchant banking firm

- for PAI : private equity firm

- for United Biscuits : production and sale of sweet and savoury biscuits, cakes and snacks.

3. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 and of paragraph 5, subparagraph (b) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [3].

4. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission

signed Philip LOWE

Director General

[1] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004 p. 1

[2] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004 p. 1

[3] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005 p.32
