# Decisão da Comissão, de 20/07/2006 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.4278 - PAI PARTNERS SAS / AMEC SPIE SA) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Brussels, 20.07.2006

SG-Greffe(2006) D/204100

To the notifying party

Dear Madam, dear Sir,

Subject: Case No. COMP/M.4278 - PAI PARTNERS/AMEC SPIE

Notification of 20.06.2006 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [1]

Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union No. C148, 24.06.2006, p. 35

1. On 20.06.2006, the Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 by which PAI Partners S.A.S. (“PAI”, France), acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation control of the whole of AMEC SPIE S.A. (“AMEC SPIE”, France), currently owned by AMEC plc, via purchase of shares.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are :

- For PAI: private equity fund;

- For AMEC SPIE: provision of multi-technical regional services and provision of multi-technical services to the nuclear, oil & gas, communications, rail and IT sectors.

3. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 and of paragraph 5, subparagraph b, of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [2].

4. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission

signed Philip LOWE

Director General

[1] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004 p. 1

[2] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005 p.32
