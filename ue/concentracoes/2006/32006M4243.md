# Decisão da Comissão, de 13/07/2006 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.4243 - INTERNATIONAL AUTOMOTIVE COMPONENTS GROUP / LEAR CORPORATION) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Brussels, 13.07.2006

SG-Greffe(2006) D/203992

To the notifying party

Dear Madam(s) and/or Sir(s),

Subject: Case No. COMP/M.4243 – International Automotive Components /Lear Corporation (European Interior Business)

Notification of 14/06/2006 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [1]

Publication in the Official Journal of the European Communities No. C 147, 23.06.2006, page 8.

1. On 14/06/2006, the Commission received a notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 by which International Automotive Components Group LLC, (“IAC”, USA, controlled by WL Ross & Co), acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation control of Lear Corporation’s European Interior System Division (“LEI”, part of the Lear Group, USA ), by way of purchase of assets.

The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

- IAC: automotive interior components and systems;

- LEI: automotive interior components and systems.

2. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 and of paragraph 5, subparagraph c, of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 [2].

3. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission,

signed Philip LOWE

Director General

[1] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004 p. 1.

[2] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005 p.32
