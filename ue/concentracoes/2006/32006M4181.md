# Decisão da Comissão, de 18/04/2006 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.4181 - BLACKSTONE / CENTER PARCS (UK) GROUP) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

| | Brussels, 18/04/2006

SG-Greffe(2006) D/202089

To the notifying party

Dear Madam(s) and/or Sir(s),

Subject: Case No. COMP/M.4181–BLACKSTONE/CENTER PARCS (UK) GROUP

Notification of 10/03/2006 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [1] Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union No. C 67, 18/03/2006, page 29

1. On 10/03/2006, the Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 by which the Blackstone Group (“Blackstone”, USA) acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation control of the whole of the undertaking Center Parcs (UK) Group Plc (“Center Parcs UK”,UK) by way of public bid announced on 9 March 2006.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are :

- for Blackstone : private merchant banking, financial advisory services, private equity investing and property investment;

- for Center Parcs UK : operates four residential holiday villages in the UK and one spa in the UK.

1. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 and of paragraph 5, subparagraph (b) or (c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004[2].

2. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission

Signed Neelie KROES Member of the Commission

[2] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005 p.32
