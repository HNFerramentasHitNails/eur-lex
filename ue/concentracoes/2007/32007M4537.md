# Decisão da Comissão, de 16/02/2007 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.4537 - LBO FRANCE / CONSOLIS) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Brussels, 16.02.2007

SG-Greffe(2007) D/200718

To the notifying party

Dear Madam(s) and/or Sir(s),

Subject: Case No. COMP/M.4537 – LBO France/Consolis

Notification of 15.01.2007 pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [1]

Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union No C 19, dated 27/01/2007, page. 17.

1. On 15.01.2007, the Commission received a notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the aforementioned Council Regulation by which the undertaking LBO France Gestion SAS ("LBO France", France) acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation control of the whole of Consolis SAS ("Consolis", France) by way of purchase of shares.

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

- for LBO: investment fund;

- for Consolis: design and manufacture of prefabricated concrete elements.

3. After examination of the notification, the Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 and of paragraph 5 (b) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 [2].

4. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.

For the Commission

signed Philip LOWE

Director General

[1] OJ L 24, 29.1.2004 p. 1

[2] OJ C 56, 05.3.2005 p.32
