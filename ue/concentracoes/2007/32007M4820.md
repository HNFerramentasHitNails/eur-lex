1.    On 08/08/2007, the Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant
      to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 by which the undertaking Cucina
      Acquisition (UK) Limited, controlled by Bain Capital Investors, LLC ('Bain Capital',
      USA), acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Council Regulation control of the
      whole of the undertaking Brake Bros Holding I Limited ('Brake Bros', United Kingdom) by
      way of purchase of shares.

      The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

      - for Bain Capital: private equity investment firm,

      - for Brake Bros: foodservice distribution.

     operation falls within the scope of Council Regulation (EC) No. 13 9/2004 and of paragraph
     5, subparagraph b of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of
                                                                        2
     certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004 •

3.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the Commission has decided
     not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the common market
     and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6( 1)(b) of
     Council Regulation (EC) No. 139/2004.
