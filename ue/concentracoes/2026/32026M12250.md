           Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation

Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 27 January 2026, the European Commission received notification of a
       proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which
       Talisman Partners 2 Pty Ltd (‘Talisman’, Australia), Mercuria Energy Group
       Limited (‘Mercuria’, Bermuda), controlled by Mercuria Energy Group Holding
       Ltd. (Cayman Islands), and IMEA Fitzroy Pty Ltd (‘IMEA’, Australia), ultimately
       controlled by ITOCHU Corporation (‘ITOCHU’, Japan), will acquire within the
       meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over
       Fitzroy (CQ) Pty Ltd (‘Fitzroy CQ’, Australia) by way of purchase of shares. (3)


(1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2026/822, 4.2.2026.
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      Talisman is active in the provision of mining support services, including
              contracting and consulting services for mines located in Australia,
       –      Mercuria is active in global energy and commodities trading, as well as
              logistics,
       –      IMEA is active in coal mine investments. ITOCHU is a trading company
              active globally in domestic trading, import/export activities, and overseas
              trading of various products (including textile, machinery, metals, minerals,
              energy, chemicals, food, information and communications technology, and
              financial).
(3)    Fitzroy CQ is active in coal exploration and mining. Fitzroy CQ focuses mostly
       on metallurgical coal. It operates coal mines in the Rangal Coal Measures in the
       Bowen Basin of Central Queensland (Australia).
(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(b) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
