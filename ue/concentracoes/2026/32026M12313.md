(1)     On 17 April 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
        concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which KKCG
        Real Estate Group a.s. (‘KKCG’, Czechia) and Rockaway Group a.s.
        (‘Rockaway’, Czechia) will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4)
        of the Merger Regulation joint control of a newly created joint venture Heršpice
        JVCo s.r.o. (‘JV’, Czechia) by way of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      KKCG is a private investment group primarily active in i) entertainment
               and games, ii) oil, natural gas and engineering, iii) real estate and iv)
               technology,
        –      Rockaway is solely controlled by Mr Jakub Havrlant. Through Rockaway
               Mr Havrlant controls other entities active in i) e-commerce, ii) financial
               technologies, iii) media and book publishing, iv) venture capital, v) defence

        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of

        certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
