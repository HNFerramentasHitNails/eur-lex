           Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation

           Economic Area (2)



Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 23 March 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Advent
       International, L.P. and/or its affiliates (‘Advent’, United States), Arendals
       Fossekompani ASA (‘AFK’, Norway) and TA Associates Management, L.P.
       (‘TA’, United States), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and
       Article 3(4) of the Merger Regulation, joint control over the whole of the
       undertaking Volue AS (‘Volue’, Norway), currently jointly controlled by Advent
       and AFK by way of purchase of shares. (3)


(1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2026/2056, 1.4.2026.
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       (a)    Advent is a private equity investor based in Boston, United States. Advent
              focuses on the acquisition of equity stakes and the management of
              investment funds in various sectors, including healthcare, industrial,
              technology, retail, consumer and leisure, and business and financial
              services,
       (b)    AFK is a Norwegian industrial investment company combining industrial,
              technology and capital markets expertise to identify value creation
              opportunities. AFK invests in forward-looking industries such as 3D
              printing, satellite services, energy storage, software, and digitalisation. AFK
              is also a producer of renewable hydropower,
       (c)    TA is the registered investment adviser to the TA funds. TA generally
              invests in five core sectors: technology, financial services, healthcare,
              consumer, and business services industries in North America, Europe and
              Asia,
       (d)    Volue is a software publisher active in the energy sector. Volue supplies
              software used exclusively by energy producers to optimise the energy-
              specific aspects of their operations.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
