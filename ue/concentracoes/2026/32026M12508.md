           Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation
           (EC) No 139/2004 (1) and Article 57 of the Agreement on the European
           Economic Area (2)



Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 13 July 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which İGA
       Holding A.Ş. (‘IGA Holding’, Türkiye), controlled jointly by Kalyon Havacılık
       ve İnşaat A.Ş. (Türkiye) and Cengiz İnşaat Sanayi ve Ticaret A.Ş. (Türkiye), and
       Unifree Duty Free İşletmeciliği Anonim Şirketi (‘Unifree’, Türkiye), controlled
       jointly by Gebr. Heinemann SE & Co. KG (Germany) and a group of individuals
       will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger
       Regulation joint control of a newly created company (‘Target JV’, Türkiye). The
       concentration is accomplished by way of purchase of shares in a newly created
       company constituting a joint venture. (3)



(1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2026/4062, 23.7.2026.
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      IGA Holding conducts holding activities by holding shares of its
              subsidiaries operating at Istanbul Airport, including the company that acts
              as the operator of Istanbul Airport,
       –      Unifree is, together with its subsidiaries and joint ventures, engaged in duty-
              free/travel value retail store activities worldwide, but primarily in Türkiye.
(3)    The Target JV will be operating travel retail stores located at Istanbul Airport as
       well as conducting travel retail activities at (other) airports outside of the EEA.
(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
