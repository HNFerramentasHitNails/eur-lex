(1)     On 29 April 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
        concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which SPIE
        Germany Switzerland Austria GmbH (‘SPIE DACH’, Germany), controlled by
        SPIE SA (‘SPIE’, France) intends to acquire, within the meaning of Article
        3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation, sole control over the whole of ROFA Industrial
        Automation AG (‘ROFA’, Germany). The concentration is to be accomplished
        by way of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      SPIE DACH is a provider of technical services for buildings, facilities and
               infrastructure in Germany and Central Europe. It is solely controlled by
               SPIE, a European provider of multi-technical services in the areas of energy
               and communications,
        –      ROFA provides industrial automation solutions and engineering services
               primarily for the automotive and warehousing and logistics sectors.
(3)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
