       joint control of a greenfield joint venture (‘Target JV’) by way of purchase of
       shares. (3)
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      Plenitude is a company active in the production of electricity from
              renewable sources, in the retail sale of electricity and natural gas to both
              domestic and non-domestic customers, in the electric mobility sector, and
              in the provision of energy efficiency services,

(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
