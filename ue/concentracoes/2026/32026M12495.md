Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 15 June 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
        concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Hong
        Kong Aircraft Engineering Company Limited (‘HAECO’, Hong Kong), Toyota
        Tsusho Corporation (‘TTC’, Japan), Japan Airlines Co., Ltd. (‘JAL’, Japan) and
        Sun Group Corporation (‘Sun Group’, Vietnam), intend to acquire within the
        meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over
        HAECO Vietnam (Aircraft Engineering) Company Limited (‘JVC’, Vietnam).
        The concentration is to be accomplished by way of purchase of shares in a newly
        created company constituting a joint venture. (3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      HAECO is a provider of maintenance, repair and overhaul services for
               commercial aircraft, mainly providing its services in Asia. HAECO is part
               of the Swire Group, a diversified global corporation, with businesses
               encompassing property, beverages and food chain, aviation, marine, as well
               as trading and industrial activities,
        –      TTC is a trading company operating globally in the metals, parts and
               logistics, automotive, machinery, energy and projects, chemicals and
               electronics, and food and consumer services sectors,
        –      JAL serves as the national flag carrier of Japan. Its main business activities
               are air passenger services, air cargo transportation, aircraft maintenance
               ground support services, ancillary aviation and travel services,
        –      Sun Group is an investment company active in real estate, infrastructure and
               aviation. Sun Group only has business activities in Vietnam.
(3)     JVC will be operating an aircraft maintenance facility in Vietnam, to provide
        heavy maintenance services for commercial aircraft in Vietnam. It will not have
        any business activities and/or assets within the EEA.
(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
        concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
