       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Morgan
       Stanley Infrastructure Inc. (“MSI”, United States), controlled by Morgan Stanley
       (United States), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger
       Regulation sole control over Nicollin SAS (“Nicollin”, France), Société
       Méditerranéenne De Nettoiement SAS (“SMN”, France), Nicollin Holding
       Environnement (“NHE”, France), Nicollin Eau (France), and MP3D Groupe
       (France), all controlled by Groupe Nicollin (France), by way of purchase of
       shares. (3)
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      MSI is active in the provision of financial services, including asset
              management advice and advice of funds that invest in infrastructure assets,
       –      Nicollin, SMN, NHE are active in the operation of waste-management
              services, and Nicollin Eau and MP3D Groupe are active in the operation of
              water services.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
