4.1.2.    Geographic market definition

4.1.2.1. The Commission’s previous practice
(22)      In its previous decisional practice, the Commission found the market for the
          production and supply of BEKP to be at least EEA-wide in scope, while
          considering also a possible global market definition. It ultimately left the exact
          geographic scope of the market for the production and supply of BEKP open. (33)

4.1.2.2. The Notifying Party’s views
(23)      The Notifying Party considers the market for the production and supply of BEKP
          to be global, or at least regional, extending beyond national boundaries. (34)

4.1.2.3. The Commission’s assessment
(24)      The market investigation confirmed that BEKP is traded globally. Almost all
          tissue producers indicated that they procure BEKP from suppliers located in the
          EEA and beyond, mostly from Latin America where eucalyptus grows under more
          favourable conditions. (35) All BEKP producers in turn indicated that they supply
          BEKP in multiple regions of the world. (36)
(25)      The results of the market investigation were mixed with regard to whether prices
          for BEKP are aligned worldwide. A majority of tissue paper producers and a
          significant minority of BEKP producers indicated that prices of BEKP vary
          between the main regions of the world. (37) However, several respondents
          submitted that regional price differences depend on elements such as local
          demand, logistics and contract timing, rather than fundamentally different BEKP
          price curves, and that price evolutions in one region of the world affect how prices
          evolve in other regions. (38) As one tissue producer explained, “[p]rice difference
          in one area cannot be defined always higher or lower compared to the others.
          Price is going up and down according to local demand, stocks and momentaneous
          supply chain assessment. The trend of prices is generally the same influenced by
          global demand and offer”. (39)

          in other regions relates to environmental certifications. The Commission’s market
          investigation revealed that, unlike in most other regions, tissue producers in the

5.      COMPETITIVE ASSESSMENT
(43)    The Transaction results in a vertical relationship between, upstream, Suzano’s
        activities on the market for the production and supply of BEKP and, downstream,
        Kimberly-Clark IFP’s activities on the production and supply of tissue paper
        products market. (57)
(44)    The Transaction does not lead to any horizontally affected markets, as Kimberly-
        Clark IFP is not active in the market for the production and supply of BEKP, and,
        conversely, Suzano is not active in the EEA production and supply of tissue paper
        products market. (58)
(45)    The Transaction also results in horizontal overlaps and vertical links for a number
        of other markets. (59) However, none of these overlaps or links give rise to affected
        markets within the meaning of recital 25(g) Annex I to Regulation (EU)
        2023/914, (60) as they meet the conditions for review under point 5 or benefit from
        the flexibility clauses of point 8 of the Notice on Simplified Treatment. (61)
        Therefore, these overlaps and vertical links will not be further discussed in this
        decision.

5.4.      Input foreclosure
(71)      Input foreclosure may occur when “post-merger, the new entity would be likely
          to restrict access to the products or services that it would have otherwise supplied
          absent the merger, thereby raising its downstream rivals' costs by making it
          harder for them to obtain supplies of the input under similar prices and conditions
          as absent the merger”. (95)

5.4.1.    Notifying Party’s views
(72)      The Notifying Party submits that Suzano will not have the ability to foreclose
          downstream competitors from accessing BEKP, because (i) its upstream position
          is moderate and multiple alternative suppliers exist across the EEA; (ii) many
          large customers are not supplied by Suzano; (iii) customers can easily switch
          between suppliers due to multi-sourcing, limited switching costs, and the
          commodity nature of BEKP; (iv) BEKP represents a limited share of downstream
          production costs, restricting any impact of price increases; and (v) customers can
          substitute BEKP with other fibre types. (96)
(73)      The Notifying Party submits that Suzano will also not have the incentive to
          foreclose downstream competitors from accessing BEKP, because (i) pulp plants
          require high utilisation, and Suzano serves a diversified customer base, including
          many non-tissue producers; (ii) the Target’s limited downstream capacity
          prevents it from capturing significant diverted BEKP sales, while other tissue
          producers have substantially greater tissue production capacity; (iii) even if
          Suzano were to constrain BEKP supplies to competitors of Kimberly-Clark IFP,
          this would result in diversion of Suzano’s sales to non-tissue producers which,
          conversely, would reduce those non-tissue producers’ demand from competing
          BEKP suppliers – hence freeing up BEKP supply capacities for Kimberly‑Clark
          IFP’s downstream competitors; and (iv) Suzano has historically continued
          supplying tissue producers following its acquisition of Kimberly-Clark
          Corporation’s business in Brazil, demonstrating commitment to maintaining
          existing customer relationships. (97)

          integrating, the merged entity may gain access to commercially sensitive
          information on the upstream or downstream activities of non-integrated rivals.

5.5.1.    Notifying Party’s views
(111)     The Notifying Party submits that, even if the Transaction were to give rise to
