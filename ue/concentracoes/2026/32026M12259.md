           Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation

Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 11 December 2025, the European Commission received notification of a
       proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
       the undertakings Global Infrastructure Management, LLC (‘GIM’, United States),
       ultimately controlled by BlackRock, Inc. (United States), and MGX Fund
       Management Limited (‘MGX’, United Arab Emirates), ultimately controlled by
       Mubadala Investment Company (United Arab Emirates) and Group 42 holding
       Ltd, (United Arab Emirates), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b)
       and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of the undertakings
       Aligned Data Centers, LLC, Aligned Data Centers REIT, LLC, ADC
       International Latam Investor GIP, Inc. and ADC International Latam Investor, LP
       (collectively ‘Aligned’, United States) by way of purchase of shares. (3)


(1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2026/194, 6.1.2026.
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      GIM is a global independent infrastructure fund manager headquartered in
              New York, United States. The funds managed by GIM invest in equity and
              equity-related investments in infrastructure and infrastructure-related assets
              in the transportation, energy, water and other public service sectors,
       –      MGX is a technology investment company focused on accelerating the
              development and adoption of AI and advanced technologies through
              partnerships in the United Arab Emirates and globally. MGX invests in
              infrastructure, semiconductors and AI core technologies and applications.
       –      Aligned is a provider of sustainable and adaptive digital infrastructure.
              Aligned owns, operates and develops enterprise-class, multitenant data
              center properties. Their business focuses on offering data infrastructure,
              scalable space, power, and cooling with rapid deployment timelines and an
              emphasis on sustainability and energy efficiency. Aligned serves cloud,
              hyperscale and enterprise customers in the US and Latin America.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
