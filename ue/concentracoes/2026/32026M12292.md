           Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation

Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 14 January 2026, the European Commission received notification of a
       proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
       Aena Desarollo Internacional SME, SA (‘Aena’, Spain), controlled by Aena
       SME, SA (Spain) will acquire, through Augusta HoldCo Limited, sole control of
       Leeds Bradford International Airport Limited (‘LBA Topco’, UK), which is
       currently solely controlled by DigitalBridge Group Inc. (‘DigitalBridge’, US),
       and joint control of NIAL Group Limited (‘NCL Topco’, UK), which is currently
       jointly controlled by DigitalBridge and Newcastle Airport Local Authority
       Holding Company Limited (‘LA NCL Topco’, UK), together with DigitalBridge
       and LA NCL Topco within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger
       Regulation, by way of contract or any other means. (3)




(1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2026/703, 29.1.2026.
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      Aena is a Spanish manager of airports, including international airports
              outside of Spain,
       –      DigitalBridge is a digital infrastructure investment firm that invests across
              45 portfolio companies worldwide. Its investment focus spans digital
              infrastructure—including data centers, fiber networks, towers, and related
              connectivity assets—as well as transportation, clean mobility, and energy-
              transition infrastructure,
       –      LA NCL Topco is owned and controlled by several UK local authorities
              and is involved in the management of Newcastle International Airport,
       –      NCL Topco manages Newcastle International Airport,
       –      LBA Topco manages Leeds Bradford Airport.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(a) and (c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
       certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
