           Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation
           (EC) No 139/2004 (1) and Article 57 of the Agreement on the European
           Economic Area (2)



Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 14 July 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which Naspers
       Limited (‘Naspers’, South Africa), the parent company of Prosus N.V. (‘Prosus’,
       Netherlands) and its indirectly wholly owned subsidiary MIH B2C Holdings B.V.
       (‘MIH’, Netherlands), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the
       Merger Regulation sole control of the whole of Dante International S.A.
       (‘eMAG’, Romania), currently jointly controlled by a private individual and
       Naspers, through Prosus and MIH, by way of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      Naspers is a global consumer internet group and a multinational holding
              company. Its subsidiary Prosus is a globally active consumer internet group
              and technology investor whose portfolio companies are active in food
              delivery, eCommerce, fintech, classified advertising and edtech across the
              EEA and worldwide,
       –      eMAG is a retailer and also intermediates the selling of electronics,
              cosmetics, pet shop products, and other products in Romania and with other
              operations in Bulgaria and Hungary.



(1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2026/3947, 21.7.2026.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(c) and 5(e) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
       certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
