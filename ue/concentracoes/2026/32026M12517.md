Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 6 August 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, and following a
       referral pursuant to Article 4(5) of the Merger Regulation, by which Mutares SE
       & Co. KGaA (together with its controlled subsidiaries “Mutares”, Germany),
       controlled by Mutares Management SE (Germany), will acquire within the
       meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control over the whole
       of Synthomer a.s. (“Synthomer”, Czechia), controlled by Synthomer Czech s.r.o.
       (Czechia) and ultimately a wholly-owned subsidiary of the public listed
       Synthomer PLC (United Kingdom) by way of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      Mutares is an international investment holding that focuses on the
              acquisition of parts of large corporations and medium-sized companies in
              transitional situations and is specialized in the development potential of
              usually distressed target companies within the framework of an active
              turnaround process. Mutares focuses on the following economic sectors:
              Automotive and Mobility, Engineering & Technology, Infrastructure &
              Special Industry, Goods & Services, and Chemicals & Materials;

(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2026/4442, 14.8.2026.
       –      Synthomer is a manufacturer of acrylic acids, acrylic monomers, acrylic
              dispersions and related derivatives, operating a production site in Sokolov,
              Czechia. Its products constitute raw materials and intermediate chemical
              products, which are supplied to a diversified international customer base for
              further processing into end-use applications. In addition to third-party sales,
              the business also supplies acrylic products and provides toll manufacturing
              services to its current parent group.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
