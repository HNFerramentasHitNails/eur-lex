           Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation

           Economic Area (2)



Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 24 June 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
       undertaking ERGO Private Capital Gesundheit GmbH (‘ERGO’, Germany),
       ultimately solely controlled by Münchener Rückversicherungs-Gesellschaft
       Aktiengesellschaft in München (‘Munich RE’, Germany), and the undertaking
       Saturn Holdco S.A.U. (‘Saturn’, Spain), controlled by certain funds managed by
       Oaktree Capital Holdings, LLC (‘Oaktree’, United States), which in turn is
       ultimately jointly controlled by Oaktree Capital Group Holdings GP, LLC (United
       States) and Brookfield Corporation (Canada), will acquire within the meaning of
       Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of
       a real estate housing portfolio (‘Housing Portfolio’, Spain), currently controlled
       by Oaktree by way of purchase of shares. (3)


(1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2026/3492, 1.7.2026.
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      ERGO holds and invests in domestic and foreign private equity firms and is
              part of the ERGO Group AG (Germany), an insurance group with activities
              worldwide. Munich RE is an international (re)insurance company,
       –      Saturn is engaged in holding, managing, coordinating and financing
              participations in other companies and investment structures primarily in
              Spain. Oaktree is a global investment management firm that invests in
              distressed debt, high yield bonds, convertible securities and other financial
              and real estate products. Brookfield is a global investment firm with
              activities in asset management, wealth solutions, infrastructure, energy,
              private equity, and real estate.
(3)    The Housing Portfolio will oversee ownership and leasing of residential real
       estate in certain Spanish cities, including Madrid, Benidorm and Valencia. It
       currently consists of approximately 2 500 affordable homes in Spain.
(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
