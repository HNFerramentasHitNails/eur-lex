Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 4 February 2026, the European Commission received notification of a
        proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
        Blackstone Inc. and affiliated funds (‘Blackstone’, USA) and Tinicum L.P. and
        affiliated funds (‘Tinicum’, USA) will acquire within the meaning of Article
        3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of Mahseer Holdings LLC
        (‘PennAero’, USA), which is currently controlled by Tinicum. As part of the same
        transaction, Blackstone and Tinicum will also acquire joint control of RSA
        Engineered Holding Company, Monogram Aerospace Fasteners, Inc., Martinic
        Engineering, Inc., Allfast Fastening Systems, LLC, Mac Fasteners, Inc. and TFI
        Aerospace Corporation (‘TriMas Aerospace’, USA), which are currently
        controlled by TriMas Corporation (USA). The concentration is accomplished by
        way of purchase of shares. (3)



( 1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
       on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
       replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
       terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
( 2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
( 3)   OJ C, C/2026/977, 11.2.2026.
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      Blackstone is a global alternative asset manager,
        –      Tinicum is a private investment firm, solely controlling, inter alia,
               PennAero, a manufacturer of aerospace components (e.g. structural
               fasteners, gears, latches and manifolds), specialty metals and semiconductor
               composites,
        –      TriMas Aerospace is a manufacturer of high-volume, standardised and
               semi-engineered aerospace components.
(3)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraphs 5(b) and (d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
        certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(4)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
        is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
        Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
