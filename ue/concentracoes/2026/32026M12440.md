            Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation

            Economic Area (2)



Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 21 May 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
        concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
        Kreditanstalt für Wiederaufbau (‘KfW’, Germany), owned by the Federal
        Republic of Germany and the German Federal States, and, for the proposed
        transaction, acting based on a Special Federal Mandate on behalf of the German
        Ministry of Economic Affairs and Energy (‘BMWE’) to be exercised with the
        concurrence of the German Federal Ministry of Defence (‘BMVg’), and GIAT
        Industries S.A.S. (‘GIAT’, France), ultimately solely controlled by the French
        State, intend to acquire, within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the
        Merger Regulation, joint control over the whole of KNDS N.V. (‘KNDS’,
        Netherlands), currently jointly controlled by GIAT and Wegmann & Co. GmbH
        (Germany). The concentration is to be accomplished by way of purchase of
        shares. (3)


( 1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
       on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
       replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
       terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
( 2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
( 3)   OJ C, C/2026/3010, 3.6.2026.
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      KfW is the German national promotional bank, focussing on promoting the
               improvement of economic, environmental and social conditions in
               Germany. BMWE and BMVg are German federal ministries,
        –      GIAT is a holding company ultimately held by the French State,
        –      KNDS develops, manufactures and supports a product portfolio ranging
               from highly protected wheeled vehicles through anti-aircraft and artillery
               systems to main battle tanks, infantry fighting vehicles and bridge-laying
               systems.
(3)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
        concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(4)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
        is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
        Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
