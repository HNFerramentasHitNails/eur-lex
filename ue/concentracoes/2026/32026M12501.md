Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 31 July 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
       Somnigroup International Inc. (United States) will acquire within the meaning of
       Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control of the whole of Leggett &
       Platt, Incorporated (United States). The concentration is accomplished by way of
       purchase of shares. (3)
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      Somnigroup International Inc. operates worldwide in the design,
              manufacturing, distribution, and retail of finished bedding products,
              including mattresses and bed bases. Its principal bedding brands include
              Tempur-Pedic, Tempur, Sealy, and Stearns & Foster,
       –      Leggett & Platt, Incorporated designs, manufactures and distributes
              components and products worldwide in three business segments: (i)
              bedding products, including finished mattresses, bed bases, foam material,
              and innersprings, (ii) specialized products, and (iii) furniture, flooring, and
              textile products.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
