Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 12 June 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
        concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Oak Hill
        Capital Management, LLC (“OHC”, United States), ultimately controlled by
        OHCM Management, L.P. (United States), and The Carlyle Group Inc.
        (“Carlyle”, United States), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and
        3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over Wyyerd Group Holdings, LLC
        (“WGH”, United States), ultimately controlled by Carlyle, by way of purchase of
        shares. (3)




( 1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
       on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
       replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
       terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
( 2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
( 3)   OJ C, C/2026/3365, 24.6.2026.
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      OHC: middle-market private equity firm active in North America, Europe
               and Asia, focused primarily on investing in services businesses in (i) digital
               infrastructure; (ii) financial services; and (iii) essential services,
        –      Carlyle: global investment firm, primarily active in North America, Europe,
               Asia and the Middle East, with assets under management across 3 business
               segments: (i) Global Private Equity; (ii) Global Credit; and (iii) Carlyle
               AlpInvest.
(3)     WGH is the holding company for the Wyyerd Group LLC, a group of companies
        active in constructing, deploying and operating high-speed fiber optic networks
        in the United States to provide high-speed internet access to residential, multi-
        dwelling unit and small and medium-sized business locations.
(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
        concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
        is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
        Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
