(12)      The Notifying Party concurs with the Commission’s precedents that operational

(13)      The vast majority of respondents to the market investigation that expressed a view

          because: i) different regulatory regimes apply to operational and financial leasing,
          ii) operational and financial leasing require different expertise and capabilities,
          iii) lessors generally specialise either in operational or financial leasing and iv)

          generally see them as substitutable alternatives to each other. (10)
