           Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation

           Economic Area (2)



Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 23 July 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Flamingo
       Produce Investments, Ltd. (‘Flamingo’, United Kingdom) a company belonging
       to the Flamingo Group (United Kingdom), ultimately indirectly controlled by Sun
       Capital Partners, Inc. (United States), and Fondo Nazca IV, F.C.R, (‘Nazca’,
       Spain) intend to acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the
       Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of Herbex Iberia, S.L. (‘Herbex’,
       Spain), currently solely controlled by Nazca. The transaction is to be
       accomplished by way of purchase of shares. (3)




(1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2026/4235, 31.7.2026.
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      Flamingo is a company specialized in growing, processing and supplying
              fresh cut flowers, house and garden plants, and premium produce,
       –      Nazca is a private equity fund specializing in the mid-market segment
              investing in small and medium enterprises, mostly family-owned. Nazca
              predominantly targets investments in the Spanish peninsula.
(3)    Herbex is a Spanish group specialized in the production and commercialization
       of fresh aromatic herbs and vegetables cultivated in Morocco and Southern Spain.
(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(b) and 5(c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
       certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
