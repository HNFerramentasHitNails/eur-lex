(34)      A merger can entail non-horizontal effects when it involves companies operating

          to the Commission Guidelines on the assessment of non-horizontal mergers under
          the Council Regulation on the control of concentrations between undertakings
          (the “Non-Horizontal Merger Guidelines”), (55) non-horizontal mergers do not
          entail the loss of direct competition between merging firms in the same relevant
          market and provide scope for efficiencies. However, there are circumstances in
          which non-horizontal mergers may significantly impede effective competition.
          This is in particular the case if they give rise to foreclosure. (56)

4.3.4.    Commission’s assessment
(41)      Some market participants raised concerns that the Parties would bundle their
          offering, thereby limiting sponsor choice, potentially increasing prices, slowing
          innovation, or removing access to Clario’s services. Further, a concern was raised
          in relation to Thermo Fisher being able to access competitor data and pricing.
          These concerns are addressed in the Commission’s assessment in the following
          paragraphs.
(42)      As explained above, the Transaction will combine complementary services in
          various closely related markets, i.e. CRO services (from Thermo Fisher) and e-
          Clinical trial solutions (from Clario).
(43)      As mentioned under Section 4.3.1 above, conglomerate concerns arise if three
          cumulative conditions are met. In the present case, the Commission concludes
          that the Transaction is unlikely to raise conglomerate concerns given that the
          merged entity will lack the ability to foreclose competitors on both the CRO and
