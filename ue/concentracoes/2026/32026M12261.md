Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 19 January 2026, the European Commission received notification of a
        proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
        CD&R Fund XII, L.P. (‘CD&R Fund XII’, United States) controlled by Clayton,
        Dubilier & Rice, LLC (‘CD&R’, United States), will acquire within the meaning
        of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation indirect sole control of Sealed Air
        Corporation (‘Sealed Air’, United States). The concentration is accomplished by
        way of public bid announced on 17 November 2025. (3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      CD&R Fund XII is a private equity fund, and CD&R is a private equity
               investment firm based in the United States, which originates, structures and
               frequently acts as lead equity investor in management buyouts, strategic
               minority equity investments and other strategic investments,
        –      Sealed Air is a global provider of packaging solutions that integrates
               sustainable, high-performance materials, automation, equipment and
               services. Sealed Air designs, manufactures and delivers packaging solutions
               that preserve food, protect goods and automate packaging processes.
(3)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
