Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 1 April 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
        concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which ITOCHU
        Corporation (‘ITOCHU’, Japan) and Peninsula Petroleum Limited (‘Peninsula’,
        Ireland) will acquire, within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger
        Regulation, joint control of a newly created company constituting a joint venture
        (‘JV’, Ireland). The concentration is accomplished by way of purchase of
        shares. (3)




( 1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
       on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
       replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
       terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
( 2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
( 3)   OJ C, C/2026/2135, 13.4.2026.
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      ITOCHU is active in domestic and international trading across a wide range
               of industries, including textiles, machinery, metals, minerals, energy,
               chemicals, food, general products, real estate, information and
               communication technology and finance as well as business investment in
               Japan and abroad,
        –      Peninsula is a globally active provider of marine energy services and
               solutions, including bunker supply.
(3)     The JV will provide marketing services related to ammonia bunkering supply
        operations in the European Union, Gibraltar and Morocco. It will also trade,
        supply, procure, handle logistics, and sell and purchase ammonia as marine fuel.
(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraphs 5 (b) and 5 (d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment
        for certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
        is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
        Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
