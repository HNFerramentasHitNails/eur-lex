           Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation
           (EC) No 139/2004 (1) and Article 57 of the Agreement on the European
           Economic Area (2)



Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 2 February 2026, the European Commission received notification of a
       proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which
       OPmobility Exterior Holding S.A. (‘OP’, France), controlled by Burelle S.A.
       (France), and Yanfeng Automotive Trim Systems CO., Ltd. (‘Yanfeng Holding’,
       China) controlled by Shanghai Automotive Industry (Group) Co., Ltd. (‘SAIC’,
       China), will, through their joint venture Yanfeng Plastic Omnium Automotive
       Exterior Systems Co., Ltd. (China), acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b)
       and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of the whole of the undertakings
       HBPO Shanghai Co., Ltd. (‘HBPO Shanghai’, China), HBPO Auto Components
       (Shanghai) Co., Ltd. (‘HBPO Auto Components’, China), HBPO Beijing Co.,
       Ltd. (‘HBPO Beijing’, China) and Shanghai Yanfeng Automotive Exterior
       Systems Co. Ltd. (‘Shanghai Yanfeng’, China), by way of purchase of shares. (3)



(1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2026/896, 9.2.2026.
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      OP is active in the development, manufacturing and supply of automotive
              components, systems and modules to original equipment manufacturers
              mainly for light vehicles,
       –      Yanfeng Holding is active in the production and supply of automotive
              interior and exterior products, seating, electronics, and safety systems. It is
              part of SAIC, which is active in the development, manufacturing and supply
              of vehicles, components, and vehicle-related trade and finance services.
(3)    The business activities of HBPO Shanghai, HBPO Auto Components, and HBPO
       Beijing (all three currently controlled by OP) are the manufacturing of automotive
       front-end modules (‘FEM’) and active grille shutters (‘AGC’) almost exclusively
       in China. The business activities of Shanghai Yanfeng (currently controlled by
       Yanfeng Holding) are the production of FEM products for passenger vehicles in
       China.
(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
