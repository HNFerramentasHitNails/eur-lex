(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      TTE ELF is the historical holding company of TotalEnergies for its
              Exploration & Production activities in the UK. TotalEnergies is a global
              integrated energy company that produces and markets energy, including oil
              and biofuels, natural gas, biogas and low-carbon hydrogen, renewables, and
              electricity. It will contribute its upstream oil and gas business in the UK
              Continental Shelf to Neo Next JV,
       –      Repsol UK is a holding company for oil and gas exploration and production
              companies operating in the UK Continental Shelf. Repsol is a global
              integrated energy company active in the oil, gas and electricity industries.
              Repsol’s business also includes a division for renewable energy, including
              renewable fuels,
       –      Neo Energy is a holding and investment company for oil and gas
              exploration and production companies in the UK Continental Shelf,
              managed by HitecVision. HitecVision is a leading provider of institutional
              capital to Europe’s energy industry, helping build successful companies
              within energy production, infrastructure, and related businesses,
       –      Neo Next JV is active in the upstream oil and gas business in the United
              Kingdom Continental Shelf.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
