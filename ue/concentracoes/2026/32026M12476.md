Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 20 July 2026, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Verdane
       IoT TopCo AS (“Verdane”, Sweden), ultimately controlled by Verdane Advisors
       Holding SAS (Norway), which in turn is controlled by its managing partner
       Bjarne Lie (Norway), and Telenor ASA (“Telenor”, Norway), will acquire within
       the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of
       Telenor Connexion AB (“Connexion”, Sweden), currently solely controlled by
       Telenor, and Verdane will acquire sole control of BLDNG.ai AS (“BLDNG”,
       Norway), currently solely controlled by Telenor, by way of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       —      Verdane: growth equity investment firm active mainly in technology-
              enabled businesses in Europe,

      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2026/4138, 29.07.2026.
       —       Telenor: telecommunications operator, providing mobile and fixed
               telecommunications services and TV distribution in Norway, Sweden,
               Denmark and Finland, as well as mobile telecommunications services in
               Asia,
       —       BLDNG: building analytics platform that uses internet-of-things data to
               optimize usage, energy efficiency and sustainability outcomes for property
               operators and tenants.
(3)    Connexion provides managed internet-of-things connectivity service solutions to
       large global customers.
(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5 (d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
