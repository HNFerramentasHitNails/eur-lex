# Decisão da Comissão, de 08/03/2018 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.8827 - APOLLO MANAGEMENT/ CBR) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

| [imagem] | EUROPEAN COMMISSION |
|---|---|

Brussels, 8.3.2018

C(2018) 1553 final

To the notifying party

Subject:Case M.8827 - APOLLO MANAGEMENT/ CBR Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 [^1] and Article 57 of the Agreement on the European Economic Area [^2]

Dear Sir or Madam,

1.On 13 February 2018, the European Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which Apollo Capital Management, LP ("Apollo", United States of America), acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of the undertaking CBR Fashion Holding GmbH ("CBR", Germany), by way of purchase of shares. [^3]

2.The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

- Apollo: management of several investment funds that invest globally in companies and debt issued by companies in various businesses such as chemical, cruise line, hospital, security, financial services and glass packaging;

- CBR: design, wholesale and retail of women's apparel.

3.After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of paragraph 5(c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. [^4]

4.For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.

For the Commission

(Signed) Johannes LAITENBERGER Director-General
