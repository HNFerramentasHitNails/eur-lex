# Decisão da Comissão, de 05/06/2018 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.8882 - KENNEDY WILSON / AXA / JV) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

PUBLIC VERSION

To the notifying parties:

Subject:Case M.8882 - KENNEDY WILSON / AXA / JV Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 [^1] and Article 57 of the Agreement on the European Economic Area [^2]

Dear Sir or Madam,

1.On 04 May 2018, the European Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the undertakings Kennedy Wilson Holdings, Inc. ('Kennedy Wilson', United States) and AXA S.A. ('AXA', France) acquire, within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation, joint control of a newly created joint venture that will be active in real estate services in Ireland, by way of purchase of shares in a newly created company constituting a joint venture. [^3]

2.The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

– for Kennedy Wilson: real estate investment, development and management;

– for AXA: life, health and other forms of insurance, as well as investment management.

3.After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of paragraph 5 (b) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. [^4]

4.For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.

For the Commission

(Signed) Johannes LAITENBERGER Director-General
