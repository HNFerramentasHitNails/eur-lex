# Decisão da Comissão, de 13/04/2018 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.8826 - ELIA SYSTEM OPERATOR / EUROGRID INTERNATIONAL) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

| [imagem] | EUROPEAN COMMISSION |
|---|---|

Brussels, 13.04.2018

C(2018) 2332 final

To the Notifying Party

Subject:Case M.8826 - ELIA SYSTEM OPERATOR / EUROGRID INTERNATIONAL Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 [^1] and Article 57 of the Agreement on the European Economic Area [^2]

Dear Sir or Madam,

1.On 15 March 2018, the European Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which Elia System Operator N.V./S.A. ("Elia", Belgium) acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control of the whole of Eurogrid International CVBA ("Eurogrid", Belgium) by way of purchase of shares [^3] . Eurogrid is currently jointly controlled by Elia and IFM Investors Pty Ltd.

2.The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

– for Elia: company owning, operating, maintaining and developing Belgium's high- and extra-high-voltage grid;

– for Eurogrid: company investing in electric utility-related companies and providing support services to its customers. Eurogrid owns 100% of 50Hertz Transmission GmbH, one of the four TSOs in Germany.

3.After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of paragraph 5(b) and 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. [^4]

4.For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.

For the Commission (Signed)

Johannes LAITENBERGER Director-General
