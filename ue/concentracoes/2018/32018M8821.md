# Decisão da Comissão, de 19/03/2018 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.8821 - ADVENT INTERNATIONAL / CIRCET GROUPE) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

Brussels, 19.03.2018

C(2018)1761 final

PUBLIC VERSION

To the notifying party :

Subject:Case M.8821 – ADVENT INTERNATIONAL / CIRCET GROUPE Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 [^1] and Article 57 of the Agreement on the European Economic Area [^2]

Dear Sir or Madam,

1.On 22 February 2018, the European Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which the undertaking Advent International Corporation (‘Advent’, USA) acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control over Circet Groupe (‘Circet’, France), by way of purchase of shares. [^3]

2.The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

-for Advent: US-based private equity investor sponsoring funds that invest globally;

-for Circet: mid-sized French company operating in telecommunications infrastructures services as well as in the distribution of mobile telephony products and service, through its affiliate Circet Réseaux.

3.After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of paragraph 5(c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. [^4]

4.For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.

For the Commission

(Signed) Johannes LAITENBERGER Director-General
