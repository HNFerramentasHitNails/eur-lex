# Decisão da Comissão, de 24/01/2017 relativa à compatibilidade com o mercado comum de uma operação de concentração (Processo no COMP/M.8311 - ALTOR FUND IV / TRANSCOM WORLDWIDE AB) com base no Regulamento (CE) n. 139/2004 do Conselho (Apenas faz fé o texto em língua inglesa)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

Brussels, 24.01.2017

C(2017) 459 final

PUBLIC VERSION

To the notifying party:

Subject:Case M.8311 – ALTOR FUND IV / TRANSCOM WORLDWIDE AB Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 [^1] and Article 57 of the Agreement on the European Economic Area [^2]

Dear Sir or Madam,

1.On 23 December 2016, the European Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which Altor Fund IV (‘Altor’, Sweden) acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation control of the whole of Transcom WorldWide AB (‘Transcom’, Sweden) by way of purchase of shares. [^3]

2.The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

— for Altor: private equity investment in a variety of economic sectors with a focus on the mid-market segment of the Nordic region,

— for Transcom: provision of outsourced customer services and support to third party customers through call centres, active globally.

3.After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of paragraph 5(b) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. [^4]

4.For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.

For the Commission

(Signed) Johannes LAITENBERGER Director-General
