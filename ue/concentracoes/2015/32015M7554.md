## Texto

EUROPEAN COMMISSION

Brussels, 17.4.2015

C(2015) 2657 final

To the notifying party:

Dear Sirs,

Subject: Case M.7554 - XL GROUP/ CATLIN GROUP

Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation (EC) No 139/20041 and Article 57 of the Agreement on the European Economic Area2

1. On 20 March 2015, the European Commission received notification of a proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which XL Group plc ('XL Group', Ireland) acquires within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation control of the whole of Catlin Group Ltd ('Catlin', Bermuda) by way of purchase of shares.3

2. The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

• for XL Group: providing property, casualty and specialty insurance and reinsurance products to industrial, commercial and professional firms, insurance companies and other enterprises on a worldwide basis.

• for Catlin: providing specialty property and casualty insurance and reinsurance, as well as life insurance, mostly to professional and business customers worldwide.

3. After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of paragraph 5(c)(i) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

4. For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.

For the Commission

(Signed)

Alexander ITALIANER

Director-General
