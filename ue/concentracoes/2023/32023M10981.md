Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 15 December 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Itochu
      Corporation (“Itochu”, Japan), Tokyo Century Corporation (“Tokyo Century”, Japan)
      and Hitachi Construction Machinery CO., Ltd (“HCM”, Japan), will acquire within
      the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of
      ZAXIS Financial Services Americas, LLC, a newly created company constituting a
      joint venture (the “JV”).3

2.   The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

     −    Itochu is a trading company for various products from across a wide range of
          industries,

     −    Tokyo Century provides a variety of financing services, including financial
          leasing services, operating leasing services and installment sales with regard to a
          variety of equipment,

     −    HCM manufactures and sells construction machinery, transportation machinery
          and other machines and devices.

3.   The business activities of the JV will be the offering of financing services related to
     HCM-products to dealers and retail customers, as well as financial services related to
     non-HCM-products upon request of its clients.

4.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
     paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of
     certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. 4

5.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
     compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
     the EEA Agreement.
