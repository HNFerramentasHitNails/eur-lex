Dear Sir or Madam,

1.     On 12 October 2023, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Mobilize
       Lease & Co S.A.S (‘Mobilize Lease’, France), controlled by Renault S.A., will
       acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control of
       Mobility Concept GmbH, DFD Deutscher Fahrzeugdienst GmbH and MS Mobility
       Solutions GmbH (together ‘MeinAuto-Group’, Germany) by way of purchase of
       shares.3

2.     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

       −    Mobilize Lease: mainly active in vehicle operational leasing and fleet
            management services,

       −    MeinAuto-Group: vehicle operational leasing and intermediation services for
            vehicle purchases.

3.     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
