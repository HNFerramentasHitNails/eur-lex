       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
       undertakings KKR & Co. Inc. (‘KKR’, United States) and Singapore
       Telecommunications Limited (‘Singtel’, Singapore), will acquire within the meaning
       of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of
       the undertaking ST Dynamo Investment Holdings Pte. Ltd. (‘ST Dynamo Investment’,
       Singapore) by way of purchase of shares.3
