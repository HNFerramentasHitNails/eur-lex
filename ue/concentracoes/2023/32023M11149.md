Dear Sir or Madam,

1.     On 31 August 2023, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which the
       undertakings Mitsui Chemicals, Inc. (‘Mitsui Chemicals’, Japan) and Asahi Kasei
       Corp. (‘Asahi Kasei’, Japan) intend to acquire within the meaning of Articles 3(1)(b)
       and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the undertaking Mitsui
       Chemicals Asahi Life Material Co., Ltd. (the JV, Japan), a newly created company
       constituting a joint venture, by way of purchase of shares.3

2.     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

       − Mitsui Chemicals is a multinational Japanese company engaged in the production
         and sale of chemical products worldwide. Mitsui Chemicals develops and sells
         a wide range of specialty products for a broad range of applications, including
         hygiene, industry, oil absorption, automotive interiors, civil engineering and
         filters, among others,

       developing fiber products, chemicals and electronic related materials. It operates
       in the material, homes, and health care business sectors.

3.   The JV will engage in the research and development, manufacture, marketing, sale
     and technical service of nonwoven fabrics made from spunbond process and from
     meltblown process, nonwoven filter systems and film and shape holding fibers. It will
     carry on the operations that both Asahi Kasei and Mitsui Chemicals run in Japan and
     in Thailand for the production, development and sale of nonwovens products.

4.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
     paragraph 5 (a) and 5 (c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for
     treatment of certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

5.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
     compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
     the EEA Agreement.
