Dear Sir or Madam,

1.     On 30 August 2023, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Ingka Holding
       B.V. (‘Ingka’, the Netherlands), and Morsano B.V. (‘Morsano’, the Netherlands),
       intend to acquire, within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and Article 3(4) of the Merger
       Regulation, joint control over the whole of Mopet Beheer B.V. (‘Mopet’, the
       Netherlands), currently controlled by Morsano. The concentration is to be
       accomplished by way of purchase of shares.3

2.     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

       -   Ingka’s business can generally be divided into three main divisions:

           (a) IKEA Retail, consisting of the operation of IKEA stores as a franchisee of the
           IKEA concept, as well as commerce via its online sales channels,

           (b) Ingka Centres, which creates and operates shopping centres around the world,
