Dear Sir or Madam,

1.     On 6 October 2023, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
       undertakings KUBIKx GmbH ("KUBIKx"), Germany, controlled by Schmitz
       Cargobull AG, VEROO GmbH ("VEROO"), Austria, a group company of "WALTER
       GROUP", i.e., the corporate group under control of AKF-Privatstiftung, Austria, that
       also includes WaVe-X Holding GmbH ("WaVe-X"), Austria, and 9.5 Magnitude
       Ventures NV ("9.5. Ventures"), Belgium, being a European Venture Capital Fund,
       will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation
       joint control over the whole of the undertaking Heylog GmbH (“Heylog” or the
       “Target”), Austria, controlled pre-Transaction by KUBIKx and 9.5 Ventures through
       the purchase of shares.3

2.   The business activities of the undertakings concerned and the joint venture are the
     following:

     −    KUBIKx specializes in the development of digital solutions in the field of fleet
          management as well as for transport and logistics processes,

     −    VEROO engages in the development of IT and software solutions and renders
          consultancy services, in particular in the area of freight. WaVe-X was founded in
          December 2022 and serves as WALTER GROUP's venture capital investment
          vehicle. Its sole purpose is the founding and managing of companies and
          participations. Upon completion of the transaction, VEROO will transfer its
          newly acquired shares in Heylog group-internally to WaVe-X,

     −    9.5 Ventures is a European Venture Capital Fund that partners with corporates to
          create or invest in tech-based (corporate) startups,

     −    Heylog is a telecommunication service provider startup in the transport sector and
          engages in the development, manufacturing, and commercialization of a cloud-
          based software platform.

3.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
     paragraph 5(b) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of
     certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
     compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
     the EEA Agreement.
