Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 20 January 2023, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Omron
      Corporation (‘Omron’, Japan) and Kirin Brewery (‘Kirin’, Japan) intend to acquire
      within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control
      of Kirin Techno-System Company (‘KTS’, Japan), currently solely controlled by
      Kirin. The concentration is accomplished by way of purchase of shares.3

2.    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

      −    Omron is a Japanese manufacturer that develops and globally supplies control
           equipment, factory automation systems, electronic components, ticket vending
           machines and medical equipment,

3.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
     paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of
     certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
     compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
