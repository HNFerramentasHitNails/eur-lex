Dear Sir or Madam,

1.   On 10 November 2023, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
     concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Smartseller
     GmbH & Co. KG (“smartseller”, Germany) will acquire together with Flughafen
     Nürnberg Service Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung (“FNSG”, Germany) joint
     control of Smartseller Flughafen Nürnberg GmbH & Co. KG (“smartseller FN”,
     Germany) currently under FNSG’s sole control, within the meaning of Articles 3(1)(b)
     and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation by way of purchase of shares.3

2.   The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

     −     Smartseller operates duty free/travel value shops at the airports of
           Münster/Osnabrück, Leipzig/Halle (both in Germany) and Ljubljana (Slovenia).
           Smartseller also provides concession food services at these airports,

     −     FNSG is active in the operation of shops including an existing duty-free shop at
           the airport of Nuremberg, Germany, as well as parking facilities.

     the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
     replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The terminology
     of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
2    OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
3    Publication in the Official Journal of the European Union, OJ C, C/2023/1089, 21.11.2023.
3.   Smartseller FN will operate the duty free/travel value shop previously held by FNSG
     and provide concession food services at the airport of Nuremberg, Germany.

4.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of paragraph
     5 (b) and 5 (d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
     concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004 on the control of
     concentrations between undertakings.4

5.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
     compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
     the EEA Agreement.
