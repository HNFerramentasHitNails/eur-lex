Dear Sir or Madam,

1.     On 13 April 2023, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Ocean
       Network Express Pte. Ltd. (‘ONE’, Singapore), controlled by Ocean Network Express
       Holdings, Ltd. (Japan), a joint venture between Nippon Yusen Kabushiki Kaisha,
       Mitsui O.S.K. Lines and Kawasaki Kisen Kaisha will acquire within the meaning of
       Articles 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of International
       Transportation Inc. (‘ITI’, United States) (the ‘Transaction’), which it will hold
       together with Skipjack Terminal Holdings (‘Skipjack’, United States), ultimately
       controlled by Macquarie Asset Management (‘Macquarie’, Australia).3

           ONE operates a global container liner shipping business,

3.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that

     paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of
     certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it

     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
