Dear Sir or Madam,

1.     On 20 October 2023, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Norwegian
       Air Shuttle ASA (‘Norwegian’, Norway) and Strawberry Holding AS (‘Strawberry’,
       Norway) will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger
       Regulation joint control over the whole of a newly created joint venture (‘LoyaltyCo
       JV’, Norway) by way of transfer of assets.3

            Exchange with no controlling shareholders. It operates domestic flights in the
            Nordics and international flights from the Nordics to destinations in Europe, as
            well as some destinations in North Africa. Norwegian’s organization is divided
            into three business areas: (i) aircrafts and assets, (ii) flight operations, and (iii)
            crew,

3.   The business activities of LoyaltyCo JV will be the following: the operation of a
     common multicompany loyalty scheme intended to offer a wide range of benefits to
     members across the Nordics. The loyalty scheme will be open to join for a variety of
     companies across a variety of sectors, including companies with and/or without a
     current loyalty scheme.

4.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
     paragraph 5(c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified procedure for treatment of
     certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

5.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
     compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
     the EEA Agreement.
