Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 6 December 2022, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
      undertaking(s) Schibsted Norge AS (‘Schibsted’), controlled by Schibsted ASA (both
      Norway) and Circle K AS (‘Circle K’, Norway), controlled by Alimentation Couche

      the Merger Regulation joint control of Elton Mobility AS (‘Elton’, Norway), currently
      solely controlled by Schibsted, by way of purchase of shares.3

           Nordics and other EEA countries,
