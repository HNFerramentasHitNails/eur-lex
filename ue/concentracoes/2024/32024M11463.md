Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 15.02.2024, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Mabanaft
          GmbH & Co. KG (“Mabanaft”, Germany), controlled by Marquard & Bahls AG
          (“Marquard & Bahls”, Germany), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b)
          of the Merger Regulation sole control over the whole of the undertaking WESTFA
          Energy GmbH (“WESTFA”, Germany), controlled by Adeleon Familienholding
          GmbH (“Adeleon”, Germany), by way of purchase of shares.(3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    Mabanaft is an integrated energy company providing energy solutions for
               transportation, heating, and industrial needs. Mabanaft is part of Marquard &
               Bahls which is active in the supply, trading and logistics of energy, petroleum
               products and chemicals.
          –    WESTFA is active in trading and distribution of liquefied petroleum gas
               (“LPG”).

(3)       After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
          the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
          paragraph 5 (d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
          concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.(4)


(1)     OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty on

        replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The terminology

(2)     OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)     OJ C, C/2024/1716, 22.02.2024.
(4)     OJ C 160, 5.5.2023, p. 1 (the ‘Notice on a simplified treatment’).

      adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
