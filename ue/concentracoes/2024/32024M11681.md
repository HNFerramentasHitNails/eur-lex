Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 11 September 2024, the European Commission received notification of a
          proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
          undertakings Marguerite II SCSp (‘Marguerite II’, Luxembourg), Ze Way Invest
          SAS (‘Ze Way Invest’, France), and WIFRA SAS (‘WIFRA’, France), ultimately
          controlled by Amundi SA (‘Amundi’, France), a subsidiary of the Crédit Agricole
          SA group (‘Crédit Agricole’, France), will acquire within the meaning of
          Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of the
          undertaking Ze Energy SAS (‘Ze Energy’, France), by way of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –     WIFRA focuses on direct infrastructure investments, notably on the energy
             transition and decarbonization sector. Amundi is an asset management company
             with a portfolio of assets dedicated to the energy transition, including renewable
             energy production infrastructure, energy distribution and efficiency
             infrastructure, and consumer infrastructure. Crédit Agricole specialises in asset
             management for both institutional and retail clients, covering all asset classes,
       –     Marguerite II is an investment fund with a particular focus on the renewables,
             energy, transport, and digital sectors,
       –     Ze Way Invest provides strategic advice to companies focusing on the
             acceleration of energy transition in the low-carbon energy production and
             electric mobility sectors,
       –     Ze Energy develops, builds, and operates hybrid ‘photovoltaic + storage’ solar
             power plants.

(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
       adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
       the EEA Agreement.
