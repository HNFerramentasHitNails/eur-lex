          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Hankook
          Tire & Technology Co., Ltd. (‘Hankook Tire’, Republic of Korea) will acquire
          within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control of the
          whole of Hanon Systems Co., Ltd (‘Hanon Systems’, Republic of Korea) by way of
          purchases of shares and securities. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    Hankook Tire is a publicly traded Korean company that is primarily engaged in
               tyre manufacturing. Hankook Tire produces and sells globally tyres for various
               types of vehicles, including passenger cars, trucks, buses, and industrial
               vehicles,
          –    Hanon Systems is a publicly traded Korean company that is engaged in the
               business of manufacturing automotive thermal management system
               components. In particular, Hanon Systems’ core products are heating,
               ventilation and air conditioning modules.

(3)       After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
