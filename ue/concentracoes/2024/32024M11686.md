(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –     for Baosteel: steel manufacturing, processing and distribution, information
              technology, carbon industry and finance,
        –     for Baowu Aluminum: production, sales, research, and development of high-
              performance high-alloyed aluminium sheet and foil products and other
              aluminium and aluminium alloy deep processing products,
        –     for Kobelco: mainly engages in investments in China, provides services to Kobe
              Group's local entities in China, and carries out purchasing, sales, import, and
              export operations. Kobelco supports Kobe Group affiliates’ import and export
              of steel and machinery products in relation to the Chinese market.

(3)     The JV will be active in China in the manufacture and sale of automotive aluminium
        flat-rolled sheet, which is a high-precision aluminium product used for the
        manufacture of sheet parts (including outer, inner, and reinforcing sheets) for
        automotive car bodies.

(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
        the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
        concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
        adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
        the EEA Agreement.
