(1)       On 25 September 2024, the European Commission received notification of a
          proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
          undertaking Eighth Cinven Fund (“Cinven VIII”, United Kingdom) controlled by
          Cinven Limited (“Cinven”, United Kingdom), will acquire within the meaning of
          Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control over the whole of the
          undertaking Idealista Global, S.A. (“Idealista”, Spain) controlled by EQT Fund
          Management S.à r.l. (“EFMS”, Luxembourg), which is, in turn, wholly-owned and
          indirectly controlled by EQT AB (“EQT”, Sweden) by way of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    Cinven is a private equity business engaged in the provision of investment
               management services to a number of investment funds. The companies in
               Cinven’s portfolio of investments are active in a wide range of sub-sectors,
               mainly business services, consumer, financial services, healthcare, industrials,
               and technology, media and telecommunications,
          –    Idealista serves real estate agents, users, and other real estate market operators
               in Southern Europe (Italy, Portugal and Spain) through its online classified
               advertising platform for sellers offering real estate for sale or rent, and buyers
               looking to buy or rent. Idealista also provides other ancillary services for the real
               estate sector.
