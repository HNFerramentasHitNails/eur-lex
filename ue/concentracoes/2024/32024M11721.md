Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 23 October 2024 and following a referral pursuant to Article 4(5) of the Merger
          Regulation, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
          undertaking STX Commodities Holding B.V. (“STX”, Netherlands), through MOH
          Holding B.V. (Netherlands), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of
          the Merger Regulation sole control over the whole of the undertaking Marson B.V.
          and its subsidiaries (“Marine Olie”, Netherlands) by way of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    STX is a trader of biofuels and related products and a provider of climate
               solutions (e.g. consulting services, reduction strategies, offsetting solutions and
               value chain optimization),
          –    Marine Olie is a trader of vegetable oils and fats, and stores and handles
               technical oils and fats (mainly used as raw materials for the production of animal
               feed, biodiesel and biological heating oil).

(3)       After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
          the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
          paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
          concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
