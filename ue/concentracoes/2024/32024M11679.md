Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 15 November 2024, the European Commission received notification of a
          proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
          Apollo Global Management, Inc. (‘Apollo’, USA), via its subsidiary AP Town
          Holdings S.à r.l. (Luxembourg), Clessidra Private Equity SGR S.p.A. (‘Clessidra’,
          Italy), controlled by Italmobiliare Investment Holding S.p.A. (‘Italmobiliare’, Italy),
          and Human Company S.r.l. (‘Human Company’, Italy) will acquire within the
          meaning of Articles 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of HU
          Holding S.r.l. (‘HU Holding’, Italy), currently controlled by Human Company, by
          way of purchase of shares. (3)

Commission européenne/Europese Commissie, 1049 Bruxelles/Brussel, BELGIQUE/BELGIË – Tel. +32 22991111
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –     Apollo is a high-growth global alternative asset manager. It offers investment
             management and financial planning services to individuals and institutional
             investors,
       –     Clessidra invests in industrial goods and services, financial services, consumer
             products and retail, business services and healthcare in Italy. It is part of
             Italmobiliare, an investment company, that owns and manages a diversified
             portfolio of equity and investments in sectors such as consumer products and
             retail, healthcare and energy,
       –     Human Company is active in the EU outdoor hospitality sector with a particular
             focus on Italy,

(3)    HU Holding operates eleven campsites in Italy, one in Luxembourg and a hostel in
       Florence. HU Holding also provides food and beverage services at sale points located
       within its facilities as well as owns a tour operator managing accommodation units
       in four third party-operated campsites in Italy.

(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
       adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
