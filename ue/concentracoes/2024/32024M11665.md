Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 15 October 2024, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Axa
          Assicurazioni S.p.A. (“AXA Assicurazioni”, Italy), controlled by AXA S.A.
          (“AXA”, France), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger
          Regulation sole control over the whole of Nobis Compagnia di Assicurazioni S.p.A.
          (“Nobis”, Italy), controlled by Nobis Holding S.p.A. (Italy), which is controlled by
          an Italian individual, by way of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    AXA Assicurazioni and its subsidiaries provide a wide range of insurance
               products and services in Italy. AXA Assicurazioni is an indirect subsidiary of
               AXA, a global insurance and asset management provider,
          –    Nobis is an insurance company providing life insurance and non-life insurance
               products, active exclusively in Italy.

(3)       After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
          the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
          paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
          concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
