Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 6 February 2024 , the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
          undertakings Aquambiente, Circular Economy Solutions, S.L.U. (‘ACES’, Spain),
          PreZero Gestión de Residuos, S.A. (‘PreZero’, Spain) and Enagás Renovable, S.A.
          (‘Enagás Renovable’, Spain), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and
          3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over PreZero Biometano, S.L. by way of
          purchase of shares in a newly created company constituting a joint venture. (3)

Commission européenne/Europese Commissie, 1049 Bruxelles/Brussel, BELGIQUE/BELGIË – Tel. +32 22991111
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –     ACES, controlled by Veolia Environment S.A. (‘Veolia’, France), is involved
              in waste and water management activities, as well as in the provision of circular
              economy services, construction activities and analysis and consulting services
              related to the foregoing. Veolia is a French group active in the following sectors:
              (i) water-related services, (ii) waste services, and (iii) energy services,
        –     PreZero is part of the German Schwarz Group and provides waste disposal and
              recycling services in the markets for the collection, sorting, treatment, recycling
              and disposal of household and commercial waste. The core business of Schwarz
              is food retailing in over 30 countries through its Lidl and Kaufland divisions,
        –     Enagás Renovable is a company focused on the development of projects in the
              field of renewable gases and decarbonization.

(3)     The business activities of the JV will be the following: to develop, construct and
        operate a plant for separately collected organic waste treatment and the production
        of biogas, which would then be purified to obtain biomethane which will, in turn, be
        sold to third parties.

(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
        the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5 (b) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
        concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
        adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
        the EEA Agreement.
