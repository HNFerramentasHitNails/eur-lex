Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 12 August 2024, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which, Gonvarri
          Corporación Financiera, S.L. (‘Gonvarri’, Spain), controlled by ACEK Desarrollo y
          Gestión Industrial, S.L. (‘ACEK Group’, Spain), intends to acquire within the
          meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control of H & R d.d.
          Družba za upravljanje z naložbami (‘Hidria’, Slovenia), currently jointly controlled
          by Gonvarri together with Inovatis d.o.o., Ladis d.o.o., Alex Luckmann and Rafis
          d.o.o. (Slovenia). The concentration is to be accomplished by way of purchase of
          shares. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    Gonvarri is active in steel services, material handling, and precision tubes and
               metal structures, while Gestamp Automoción, which like Gonvarri is also part
               of the ACEK group, is active worldwide in automotive components,
          –    Hidria produces a range of automotive and industrial products.

(3)       After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
          the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
          paragraphs 5(d) and 5(e) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
          certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
