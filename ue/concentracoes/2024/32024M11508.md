          undertakings Global Digital Integrated Solutions Company (‘Aramco Digital’, Saudi
          Arabia), controlled by Saudi Arabian Oil Company (Saudi Arabia), and LTIMindtree

          purchase of shares. (3)

               transformation of various industrial sectors in Saudi Arabia, such as energy,
               petrochemical and manufacturing,
