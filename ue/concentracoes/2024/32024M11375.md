Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 21 December 2023, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
      undertakings Blackstone Inc. (together with its affiliates “Blackstone”, United
      States), and Vista Equity Partners Management, LLC (together with its affiliates
      “Vista”, United States), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the
      Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of the undertaking Simulation
      Software Holding Company Pty Ltd, (“Energy Exemplar”, Australia), ultimate parent
      entity of the Energy Exemplar group of companies by way of purchase of shares. (3)

    of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2) OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3) OJ C, C/2024/783, 11.01.2024.

Commission européenne/Europese Commissie, 1049 Bruxelles/Brussel, BELGIQUE/BELGIË – Tel. +32 22991111
2.   The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

     – Blackstone is a global alternative asset manager. It is headquartered in the United
       States and has offices in Europe and Asia,
     – Vista is a United States-based investment firm focused on empowering and
       growing enterprise software, data and technology-enabled businesses. Vista
       controls a number of portfolio companies that are active in the provision of IT
       services such as the provision of business software,

     – Energy Exemplar is a provider of energy market modelling and analytics software
       to assist decision-making in energy planning, operations, and investment for their
       customers in the electricity, water, gas and renewables sectors. Energy Exemplar
       is an Australian company with a global presence.

3.   After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
     the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
     paragraphs 5(b) and 5(c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
     certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
     Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
     compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
     adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
     the EEA Agreement.
