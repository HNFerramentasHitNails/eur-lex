Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 6 September 2024, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which Tubacex S.A.
          (‘Tubacex’, Spain) and Mubadala Investment Company P.J.S.C. (‘Mubadala’,
          United Arab Emirates) owned by the Government of Abu Dhabi in the United Arab
          Emirates will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation
          control of part(s) of TBX NewCo Spain, S.L.U. (‘Spanish JVCo’, Spain) and TBX
          Upstream Seamless Pipes L.L.C. (‘UAE JVCo’, United Arab Emirates). The
          concentration is accomplished by way of purchase of shares and securities. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    Tubacex provides complex industrial products and services, specifically focused
               on the energy and mobility industries and specialising in advanced stainless
               steels and other corrosion-resistant alloys,
          –    Mubadala is an investment company that actively invests in a wide range of asset
               classes such as private equity, public equities, real estate and infrastructure,
               alternatives and credit.

        of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)     OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)     OJ C, C/2024/5569, 16.9.2024.

Commission européenne/Europese Commissie, 1049 Bruxelles/Brussel, BELGIQUE/BELGIË – Tel. +32 22991111
(3)    The business activities of the Spanish JVCo are the following: Providing oil country
       tubular goods (OCTG) with operations in Spain and Brazil. The UAE JVCo is not
       yet operational, but it will be providing similar services.

(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraphs 5 (b) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
       adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
       the EEA Agreement.
