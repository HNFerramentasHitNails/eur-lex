Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 16 February 2024, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which RWE
          Offshore Wind Japan Murakami-Tainai K.K. (‘RWE KK’, Japan), which is
          ultimately controlled by RWE AG (‘RWE’, Germany), Mitsui & Co., Ltd. (‘Mitsui’,
          Japan), and Osaka Gas Co., Ltd (‘Osaka Gas’, Japan) will acquire within the meaning
          of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of
          a newly created company constituting a joint venture (the ‘JV’, Japan). (3)

        of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)     OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).

Commission européenne/Europese Commissie, 1049 Bruxelles/Brussel, BELGIQUE/BELGIË – Tel. +32 22991111
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –     Mitsui is a worldwide trading, business management, and project development
             company active in many economic activities (including iron and steel products,
             mineral and metal resources, infrastructure, mobility, chemicals, energy, food
             resources, consumer services, information technology, communication, and
             corporate development).
       –     Osaka Gas is an energy company dedicated to (i) the production, distribution,
             transportation, and supply of oil and natural gas, energy, liquefied natural gas,
             as well as (ii) solutions including the development and leasing of real estate
             properties, information-processing services, sale of fine materials and carbon
             material products.
       –     RWE KK is a special purpose vehicle company established by RWE, a group of
             companies mostly active in the generation and trading of energy, to participate
             in future auctions in Japan.

(3)    The business activities of the JV will be the development, construction, and operation
       of an offshore windfarm in Japan.

(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
       adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
       the EEA Agreement.
