Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 24 October 2024, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which MML
          Growth Capital LLP (‘MML’, United Kingdom) and Apheon Management SA
          (‘Apheon’, Luxembourg) will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4)
          of the Merger Regulation joint control of The Member Company (‘TMC’, the
          Netherlands, ), and its parent company Triple B.V. (‘Triple’, ‘the Netherlands’) by
          way of purchase of shares. (3) Pre-transaction, both TMC and Triple, its parent
          company, are ultimately solely controlled by Apheon.

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    MML is a private equity company, active globally,
          –    Apheon is a private equity company, active globally.

        of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.

( 3)    OJ C, C/2024/6715, 31.10.2024.

Commission européenne/Europese Commissie, 1049 Bruxelles/Brussel, BELGIQUE/BELGIË – Tel. +32 22991111
(3)     The business activities of TMC are the following: TMC provides specialist
        consulting services with a focus on technological engineering for customers' core
        research and development processes.

(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
        the notified concentration falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
        concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified concentration and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
        adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
        the EEA Agreement.
