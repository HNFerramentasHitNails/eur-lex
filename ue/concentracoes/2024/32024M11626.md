Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 19 July 2024, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Koninklijke
          Jumbo Food Group B.V. (‘Jumbo’, Netherlands), controlled by Stichting Jumbo
          Groep (Netherlands) and Vermaat Retail B.V. (‘Vermaat’, Netherlands), controlled
          by Bridgepoint Group plc (United Kingdom), will acquire within the meaning of
          Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of the
          undertaking La Place B.V. (‘La Place’, Netherlands), controlled by Jumbo, by way
          of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    Jumbo operates supermarkets in the Netherlands and Belgium. It also sells daily
               consumer goods online through Jumbo.com;
          –    Vermaat provides foodservices in the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany. It
               also owns convenience/food stores and operates as a franchisee 41 La Place
               restaurants in the Netherlands.

        replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The terminology
        of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)     OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)     OJ C, C/2024/4776, 29.7.2024.

Commission européenne/Europese Commissie, 1049 Bruxelles/Brussel, BELGIQUE/BELGIË – Tel. +32 22991111
(3)    The business activities of the undertaking La Place are in the Netherlands and consist
       of operating its own La Place restaurants and the La Place franchise services.

(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(b) and (d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
       certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
       adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
       the EEA Agreement.
