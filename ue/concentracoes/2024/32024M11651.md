Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 16 July 2024, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Ardian
          France S.A. (France) (“Ardian”), controlled by Ardian Holding SAS, France, and
          Qatar Holding LLC (“QH"), controlled by Qatar Investment Authority ("QIA"),
          Qatar, will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger
          Regulation joint control of FGP Topco Limited (“FGP”), the ultimate parent
          company of Heathrow Airport Holdings Limited, United Kingdom, currently jointly
          controlled by Ferrovial S.A. (“Spain”) and QH. The concentration is accomplished
          by way of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    Ardian is a global private equity investor and asset manager,
          –    QH is a global investment holding company founded by QIA, the sovereign
               investment fund of the State of Qatar,

       –     FGP, through its subsidiaries, owns, manages and maintains Heathrow Airport
             in the United Kingdom, including runways, terminals, baggage systems, and
             other airport facilities, including the Heathrow Express rail service.

(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
       adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
       the EEA Agreement.
