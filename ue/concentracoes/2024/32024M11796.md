Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 30 October 2024, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
          undertakings AustralianSuper Pty Ltd (“AustralianSuper”, Australia), DigitalBridge
          Group, Inc. (“DigitalBridge”, United States) and Swiss Life Holding AG (“Swiss
          Life”, Switzerland) will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the
          Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of the undertaking DataBridge Upper
          Parent Holdings IV, LP (“DBUPH”, United States), currently jointly controlled by
          DigitalBridge and Swiss Life, by way of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –     AustralianSuper is an industry public offer superannuation and pension fund
             with investments in Australia and other countries globally,
       –     DigitalBridge is a dedicated, global-scale digital infrastructure investment firm
             that invests in five key areas: (i) data centres; (ii) cell towers; (iii) fiber networks;
             (iv) small cells; and (v) edge infrastructure,
       –     Swiss Life is a provider of life insurance, pension and financial solutions. It is
             active in the life insurance, investment and asset management sectors, and
             provides multinational corporations with employee benefit solutions,
       –     DBUPH is a provider of colocation, interconnection and managed services. Its
             business is focused on the United States.

(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
       adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
       the EEA Agreement.
