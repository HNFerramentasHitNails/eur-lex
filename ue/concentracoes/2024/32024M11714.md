Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)     On 15 October 2024, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
        concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which the
        undertaking One Rock Capital Partners, LLC (‘One Rock Capital’, United States)
        intends to acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole
        control of the undertaking Snacks Développement SAS (‘Europe Snacks’, France), by
        way of purchase of shares and securities.3

(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

        – One Rock Capital is a private equity firm headquartered in New York, United
          States, and focusing on control-stake equity investments in middle-market
          companies. One Rock Capital manages funds that invest in portfolio companies
          operating within various industries, including Chemicals & Process Industries,
          Food & Beverage Manufacturing & Distribution, Specialty Manufacturing, and
          Business & Environmental Services,

        – Europe Snacks is a company headquartered in France, that provides private label
          and co-manufacturing of savoury snacks (crisps, crackers, popcorn, etc.) in Europe,
          mainly in France, the UK and Spain.

(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5 (d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
       adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
