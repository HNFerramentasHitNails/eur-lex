Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)     On 27 September 2024, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
        concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which the
        undertaking Sonoco Products Company (‘Sonoco’, United States) intends to acquire
        within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control of the
        Eviosys Group (‘Eviosys’, Switzerland), by way of purchase of shares.3

(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

        – Sonoco is a global manufacturer of industrial and consumer packaging products,
          including in particular composite cans and rigid paper containers. In Europe, it is
          mostly active in consumer and industrial plastics, composite (paper-based) cans
          and, to a limited extent, standalone metal can ends,

        – Eviosys activities are across metal food packaging solutions, closures, promotional
          metal packaging designs, and tinplate aerosol cans. In Europe, it is active in
          packaging for food and promotional cans, standalone can ends, and aerosols.

(3)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
