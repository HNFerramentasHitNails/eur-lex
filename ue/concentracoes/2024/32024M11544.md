Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 2 July 2024, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which, Pfeifer &
          Langen GmbH & Co. KG (‘Pfeifer & Langen’, Germany) and Lauksaimniecības
          pakalpojumu kooperatīvā sabiedrība (‘LATRAPS’, Latvia) intend to acquire, within
          the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation, joint control of
          ASNS Ingredient SIA (‘ASNS’, Latvia), currently controlled by LATRAPS. The
          concentration is to be accomplished by way of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    Pfeifer & Langen primarily manufactures and supplies sugar, drinks, foodstuffs
               and meat and fish substitute products,
          –    LATRAPS is a Latvian agricultural service cooperative society, wholly owned
               by 1,195 members, and provides its members assistance in growing, developing,
               and selling crops.

(3)       The business activities of the undertaking ASNS are the following: ASNS oversees
          the development of a pea protein isolate plant in Jelgavas, Latvia, expected to be
          operational in 2027.

(4)       After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
          the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of

(3)     OJ C, C/2024/4425, 10.07.2024, C/2024/90048, 12.07.2024.
