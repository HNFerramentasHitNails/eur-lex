Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 24 September 2024, the European Commission received notification of a
          proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
          undertakings EP UK Investments Limited (‘EPUKI’, United Kingdom) ultimately
          controlled by Mr Daniel Křetínský (Czechia), and TotalEnergies Gas & Power
          Holdings UK Limited (‘TTE’, United Kingdom) controlled by TotalEnergies SE
          (France), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger
          Regulation joint control over the whole of the undertaking West Burton Flexible
          Generation Limited (‘West Burton Flexible Generation’, United Kingdom) by way
          of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)     OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).

Commission européenne/Europese Commissie, 1049 Bruxelles/Brussel, BELGIQUE/BELGIË – Tel. +32 22991111
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –     EPUKI is active in the United Kingdom (i) in the generation and wholesale
             supply of electricity, (ii) in the provision of balancing and ancillary services,
             (iii) in financial electricity trading and (iv) in the retail supply of gas,
       –     TTE supplies gas to industrial and commercial customers and is active in the
             generation and wholesale supply of electricity in the United Kingdom.

(3)    The business activities of West Burton Flexible Generation relate to the generation,
       wholesale supply of electricity and provision of balancing and ancillary services in
       the United Kingdom.

(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
       adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
       the EEA Agreement.
