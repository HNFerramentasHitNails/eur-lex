Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 15 November 2024, the European Commission received notification of a
          proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which (i)
          the undertaking Abu Dhabi Marine Business and Services Company P.J.S.C.
          (‘ADNOC’, United Arab Emirates) will acquire within the meaning of Article
          3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control of the whole of Navig8 Topco
          Holdings Inc. (‘Navig8’, Marshall Islands), which is currently wholly owned by
          Navig8 Limited (‘Seller’, Bermuda), by way of purchase of shares, with the
          exception of Navig8’s subsidiary, Integr8 Fuels Holding Inc. (‘Integr8’, Marshall
          Islands) and (ii) ADNOC and the Seller will acquire within the meaning of Article
          3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of Integr8, by way of
          purchase of shares. (3)

        of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
( 2)    OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).

Commission européenne/Europese Commissie, 1049 Bruxelles/Brussel, BELGIQUE/BELGIË – Tel. +32 22991111
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –     ADNOC is part of the ADNOC Group, which is an energy and petrochemicals
              group operating across the entire hydrocarbon value chain through a network of
              fully integrated businesses. ADNOC is wholly-owned by the Government of the
              Emirate of Abu Dhabi. It is principally active in the exploration, production,
              storage, refining, and distribution of oil and gas, as well as in the development
              of petrochemical products,
        –     The Seller is the current owner of Navig8 (which includes Integr8),
        –     Navig8 is a fully integrated provider of shipping management services,
              operating as an owner, operator and active charterer and broker of liquid bulk
              ships, and shipping pools spanning the clean and dirty tanker and chemical
              sectors. Integr8, a subsidiary of Navig8, is a marine fuel (bunker fuel) trading
              business.

(3)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
        the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
        concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(4)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
        adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
        the EEA Agreement.
