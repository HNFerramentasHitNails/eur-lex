(93)      Article 12.1 of the Greek Media Law provides that media agencies can only

          agencies from pooling volumes from different advertisers to obtain better terms

          specific. When an agency intermediates, the invoice is issued from the vendor in

          media agencies can obtain but also prevents them from using such
          bonuses/rebates to improve their commercial terms with advertisers.
