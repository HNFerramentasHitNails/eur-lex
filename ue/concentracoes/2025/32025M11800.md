5.2.    Legal framework
(16)    The Commission’s Guidelines on the assessment of horizontal mergers under the
        Merger Regulation (the ‘Horizontal Merger Guidelines’) distinguish two main
        ways in which mergers between actual or potential competitors on the same
        relevant market may significantly impede effective competition, namely non-
        coordinated effects and coordinated effects. (14)
(17)    Non-coordinated effects may significantly impede effective competition by
        eliminating the competitive constraint imposed by one merging party on the other,
        as a result of which the merged entity would have increased market power without
        resorting to coordinated behaviour. (15) The Horizontal Merger Guidelines list a
        number of factors, which may influence the rise of substantial non-coordinated
        effects from a merger, such as the large market shares of the merging firms. Not
        all of these factors need to be present to make significant non-coordinated effects
        likely and the list itself is not an exhaustive list. (16)

5.3.    Multi-role combat aircraft in Italy
(18)    In the Parties’ view, the overlap between BAES and Leonardo on the market for
        multi-role combat aircraft in Italy does not give rise to concerns because their
        products are (and have been in the past) the same and they have never been offered
        by BAES or Leonardo alone. (17)
(19)    The horizontally affected market in relation to multi-role combat aircraft in Italy
        is indeed based on pre-existing overlapping activities of BAES and Leonardo (not
        JAIEC who is not active on these markets and is not a potential competitor) with
        a combined market share of [20-30]% (BAES: [5-10]% and Leonardo:
        [10-20]%): (18) (i) sales of Tornado by Panavia Aircraft GmbH that is controlled
        by BAES, Leonardo, and Airbus Defence & Space; (ii) sales of Eurofighter by
        Eurofighter Jagdflugzeug GmbH that is controlled by BAES, Leonardo, and
        Airbus Defence & Space; and (iii) sales of F-35 where BAES, and Leonardo
        collaborate with Lockheed Martin as sub-contractors to deliver parts of each
        aircraft. In the same vein, as a result of the Transaction, BAES and Leonardo
        would again supply together within the JV. These pre-existing overlapping
        activities are not specific to the Transaction, and hence do not give rise to
        competitive concerns. The concentration will not increase the market power of
        BAES and Leonardo on these markets compared to the pre-existing situation.
(20)    In light of the foregoing, the Commission considers that the Transaction does not
        raise serious doubts as to its compatibility with the internal market as a result of
        horizontal effects in respect of the market for multi-role combat aircraft in Italy.
