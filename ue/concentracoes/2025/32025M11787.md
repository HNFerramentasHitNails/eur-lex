          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
          undertaking Ares Management Corporation (“Ares”, USA) will acquire within the
          meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control over the
          international business (excluding its operations in Greater China) of the undertaking
          GLP Capital Partners (“GCP”, Singapore) and certain of its affiliates and certain GP
          capital commitments by way of purchase of shares and assets. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    Ares is headquartered in California, United States, and it is a global alternative
               investment manager offering clients complementary primary and secondary
               investment solutions across the credit, private equity, real estate and
               infrastructure asset classes; and,
          –    GCP is a global asset management business of Global Logistics Partner. GCP is
               an alternative investment manager primarily focused on logistics real estate,
               digital infrastructure and self-storage with a focus on Asia, Europe, the United
               States and Brazil.

(3)       After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
