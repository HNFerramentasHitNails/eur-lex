Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 12 February 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which AURELIUS
          Investment Lux One S.à r.l. (‘Aurelius’, Luxembourg), belonging to Aurelius Group,
          will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole
          control over Lernia AB (‘Lernia’, Sweden), by way of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    Aurelius is active both within and outside Europe in various markets such as,
               pharmaceuticals, airline catering, retail, glass packaging and publishing,
               manufacture of power transmission products, processing of food products etc. It
               belongs to Aurelius Group, a private equity investment company and an asset
               manager holding various portfolio companies active in industry, chemistry,
               retail, business services, media and IT, and airline catering. Aurelius Group is
               active worldwide,
          –    Lernia is active in staffing and recruitment, especially in the blue-collar
               segment. It also provides Higher Vocational Education and offers support and
               coaching to unemployed individuals, in collaboration with the Swedish Public
               Employment Service. Lernia is only active in Sweden.

(3)       After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
          the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
