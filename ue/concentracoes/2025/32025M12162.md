Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 11 November 2025, the European Commission received notification of a
        proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
        the undertakings BP p.l.c. (‘bp’, United Kingdom), through its wholly-owned
        subsidiary BP Products North America Inc. (United States), and Corteva, Inc.
        (‘Corteva’, United States), through its wholly-owned subsidiary Pioneer Hi-Bred
        International Inc. (United States), will acquire within the meaning of Article
        3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of the newly established
        joint venture Corteva BP LLC (the ‘JV’, United States) by way of purchase of
        shares. (3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      bp is the parent company of an energy group globally active in the provision
               of oil, natural gas, petroleum and petrochemical products, and alternative
               energy, including the production and sale of sustainable aviation fuel,

       replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The

( 2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
( 3)   OJ C, C/2025/6253, 19.11.2025.
        –      Corteva is the parent company of an agriculture group globally active in the
               production and sale of seeds and crop protection products, including seeds
               that can be used to produce crude vegetable oil.
(3)     The JV will commercialise crude vegetable oil (oil feedstock) derived from
        specific crops (i.e., canola oilseeds and mustard oilseeds grown in the United
        States and sunflower oilseeds grown in France, Italy, and Romania), which will
        be mainly used to produce and sell sustainable aviation fuel that complies with
        Annex IX Part A (t) to Directive (EU) 2018/2001 of the European Parliament and
        of the Council as regards adding feedstock for the production of biofuels and
        biogas, as amended by Commission Delegated Directive (EU) 2024/1405 of 14
        March 2024. It will also sell oilseed meal.
(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraphs 5 (b) and 8 of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
        certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
        is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
        Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
