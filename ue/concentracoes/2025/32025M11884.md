(1)     On 19 February 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
        concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
        undertakings Hunan Yunchu Circular New Energy Technology Co., Ltd. (“Hunan
        Yunchu”, China), controlled by China Minmetals Corporation (“Minmetals”,
        China), Minmetals New Energy Materials (Hunan) Co., Ltd. (“Minmetals New
        Energy”, China), also controlled by Minmetals, Meiwa (Shanghai) Corporation
        (“Meiwa”, China), and Toyota Motor (China) Investment Co., Ltd. (“TMCI”,
        China), controlled by Toyota Motor Corporation (“Toyota”, Japan) will acquire
        within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control
        over the whole of the undertaking Hunan Yunchu Siweipu New Energy Technology
        Co. Ltd (“JV”, China) by way of purchase of shares in a newly created company
        constituting a joint venture. (3)

(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –    Hunan Yunchu (4) produces and sells energy storage products and batteries,
        –    Minmetals New Energy (5) is active in the production and sale of high-efficiency
             battery materials in China (namely of cobalt and lithium),
        –    Meiwa engages in the production and sale of petroleum products and machinery
             chemicals within China, and
        –    TMCI is the regional headquarter and investment company of Toyota in China.
             It participates in Toyota’s production and R&D investment activities in China.
             Toyota is active in the automotive industry.

(3)     The business activities of the JV will be mainly the secondary utilisation of waste
        lithium-ion power batteries.

(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
        the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
        concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (6)

(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
        adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
        the EEA Agreement.
