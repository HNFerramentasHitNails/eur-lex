Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 3 January 2025, the Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which J.C. Flowers
          & Co. LLC (“J.C. Flowers”, United States) and TowerBrook Capital Partners (U.K.)
          LLP (“TowerBrook”, United Kingdom) will acquire within the meaning of Article
          3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation joint control of the whole of Embedded Finance
          Limited (“Railsr”, United Kingdom) and Equals Group Plc (“Equals”, United
          Kingdom) by way of purchase of shares.(3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    J.C. Flowers is a private investment firm focused on investing in financial
               services,
          –    TowerBrook is a private investment management firm, which invests in various
               sectors including consumer, finance, healthcare, industrials, technology, media
               and services businesses,

(2)     OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).

       –     Railsr offers an embedded finance platform, which allows the integration of
             financial services within companies’ products or services, as well as embedded
             card products and services,
       –     Equals develops and sells scalable platforms primarily to enable businesses to
             manage money flows through its payment and card products.

(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraphs 5(b) and (c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
       certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
       adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
       the EEA Agreement.
