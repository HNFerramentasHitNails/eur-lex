Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 4 March 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which UniCredit
          S.p.A. (“UniCredit”, Italy) will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the
          Merger Regulation sole control over the whole of UniCredit Allianz Vita S.p.A.
          (“UAV”, Italy) by way of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    UniCredit is an authorized credit institution providing various banking and
               financial services in the European Union, such as retail and corporate banking,
               financial market services and other financial services,
          –    UAV is an authorized insurance company active in the provision of life
               insurance products in Italy.

(3)       After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
          the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
          paragraphs 5(d) and 5(e) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
          certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
