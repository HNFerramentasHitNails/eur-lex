        Capital Group, L.P. (‘Clearlake Capital Group’, USA) will acquire within the
        meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control of the whole of

        is accomplished by way of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

               portfolio companies active in software and technology-enabled services,
               energy and industrials, and food and consumer products,
        –      Dun & Bradstreet is a global commercial data and analytics provider that
               specializes in business credit reporting and risk assessment.
(3)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
