Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 24 July 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Brose
       Sitech Sp. z o.o. (“Brose Sitech”, Poland), ultimately controlled by Brose SE
       (“Brose Group”, Germany), will acquire, via its wholly owned subsidiary Brose
       Sitech Foam GmbH, Germany, within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the
       Merger Regulation sole control over the Proseat foam, trim and lightweight
       components business (“Proseat”), comprised of (i) Proseat Verwaltung GmbH,
       Germany, (ii) Proseat GmbH & Co. KG, Germany (which holds 100% of the
       shares in Proseat Schwarzheide GmbH, Germany), (iii) Proseat Mladá Boleslav
       s.r.o., Czech Republic, (iv) Proseat Sp. z o.o, Poland, and (v) Proseat Foam
       Manufacturing S.L.U., Spain, all currently ultimately controlled by Sekisui Kasei
       Co. Ltd., Japan, by way of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      Brose Sitech is a supplier of complete seats to the automotive industry, and
              Brose Group supplies various automotive components and systems.
       –      Proseat is a supplier of moulded foam, interior and lightweight component
              solutions to the automotive industry.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
