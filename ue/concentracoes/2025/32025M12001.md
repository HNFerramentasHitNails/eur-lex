        Mitsui & Co., Ltd. (‘Mitsui’, Japan), Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Ltd. (‘MHI’,
        Japan), and Mitsubishi Electric Corporation (‘MEC’, Japan) will acquire within
        the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of

       replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
       terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
( 2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
        Japan Leo Shachu, Inc. (‘JLS’, Japan). The concentration is accomplished by way
        of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      Mitsui is a trading house with registered offices in Japan with its geographic
               area of activity spanning Asia, the Americas, Europe, the Commonwealth
               of Independent States, Africa, the Middle East, and Oceania,
        –      MHI is engaged in the businesses of energy systems (power generation
               systems), plants and infrastructure systems (commercial ships, engineering,
               etc.), logistics, thermal and drive systems (material handling equipment,
               engines, air conditioning systems, etc.), aircraft, defence and space with its
               geographic area of activity spanning Asia, the Americas, Europe, the
               Middle East, Oceania and Africa,
        –      MEC is engaged in the businesses of public utility systems, energy systems,
               defence and space systems, factory automation systems, automotive
               equipment, building systems, air conditioning and home products, digital
               innovation, and semiconductors with its geographic area of activity
               spanning the Americas, Asia-Pacific, Europe, the Commonwealth of
               Independent States, the Middle East and Africa.
(3)     JLS develops and operates the Japan Module and HTV-XC (unmanned cargo
        transporter) for US commercial space stations, and provision of related services,
        making use of the Space Strategy Fund currently wholly owned and solely
        controlled by Mitsui.
(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
        concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
        is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
        Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
