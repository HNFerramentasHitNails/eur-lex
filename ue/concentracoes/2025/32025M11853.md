          Holdings A/S (‘Novo Holdings’, Denmark), controlled by Novo Nordisk Fonden
          (Denmark), and TA Associates Management, L.P. (together with its subsidiaries,
          ‘TA Associates’, Unites States of America), will acquire within the meaning of
          Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of
          Matrix Topco Limited (together with its subsidiaries, ‘Biocomposites’, United
          Kingdom), controlled by TA Associates, by way of purchase of shares. (3)

               assets of the Novo Nordisk Fonden through strategic investments in the life
               sciences, planetary health and related areas and through investments of a
               financial and venture capital nature in a broad portfolio of companies dedicated
               to medical and scientific research,
