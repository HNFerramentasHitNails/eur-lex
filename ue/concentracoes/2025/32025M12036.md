(1)    On 23 June 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which Platinum
       Equity Group, ultimately controlled by Platinum Equity, LLC (‘Platinum Equity
       Group’, United States) will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the
       Merger Regulation sole control over Solo Invest SAS, the holding company of
       Solo Group (‘Solo Group’, France), ultimately controlled by Akiba Holding. The
       concentration is accomplished by way of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      Platinum Equity Group is globally active in private equity and investment
              strategies, on behalf of institutional investors, in a broad range of businesses
              including information technology, telecommunications, logistics, metal
              services, manufacturing and distribution,
       –      Solo Group is active in the wholesale supply of customisable clothing and
              hardware goods to resellers, distributors and printing specialists.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
