           Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation

           Economic Area (2)



Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 25 September 2025, the European Commission received notification of a
       proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
       AAR CORP (‘AAR’, United States) and Air France, ‘societé anonyme,’ (‘Air
       France’, France), belonging to the Air France-KLM group intend to acquire,
       within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation, joint
       control over xCelle Americas JV (‘JV’, United States). (3) The concentration is to
       be accomplished by changes to the JV’s operational set up and activities. The JV
       becomes full function within the meaning of the Merger Regulation and the
       shareholding of AAR and Air France remains the same as prior to the transaction.




(1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2025/5375, 3.10.2025.
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      AAR is a global aerospace and defence supplier offering a comprehensive
              range of aviation services and products, including maintenance, repair, and
              overhaul (‘MRO’), as well as supply chain solutions,
       –      Air France is engaged in the business of passengers and cargo transportation
              together with aeronautical maintenance for airlines.
(3)    The JV is providing MRO services for nacelles in North, South and Central
       America, as well as the Caribbean.
(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
