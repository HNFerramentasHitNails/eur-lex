Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 12 February 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Cement
          Australia (Queensland) Pty Ltd (‘Cement Australia’, Australia), jointly controlled by
          Holcim (Australia) Pty Ltd (‘Holcim’, Australia), a company controlled by Holcim
          Limited (Switzerland), and Heidelberg Materials AG (‘Heidelberg’, Germany),
          intends to acquire, within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation,
          control over certain assets of the BGC Group (‘BGC Cementitious’, Australia). The
          concentration is to be accomplished by way of purchase of assets. (3)
(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

               ⎯ Cement Australia supplies cementitious products and services, including
                 bulk and packaged cement, fly ash and slag products,

               ⎯ BGC Cementitious includes assets relating to quarry, cement, concrete,
                 asphalt, transport and materials technology.

(3)       After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
          the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of

(1)     OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty on

(2)     OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)     OJ C, C/2025/1370, 24.2.2025.
