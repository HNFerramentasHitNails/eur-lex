Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 3 March 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which Sociedad
          Quimica y Minera de Chile SA (‘SQM’, Chile) and Corporación Nacional del Cobre
          de Chile (‘Codelco’, Chile), ultimately controlled by the Chilean State, will acquire
          within the meaning of Articles 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint
          control of the whole of SQM Salar SpA (‘SQM Salar’ Chile), currently controlled
          by SQM, by way of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    SQM is engaged in the production and marketing of (i) lithium and its
               derivatives; (ii) iodine and its derivatives; (iii) specialty plant nutrition
               (fertilisers); (iv) potassium and its derivatives; and (v) industrial chemicals,

        terms of conditions of lithium exploitation in the Atacama Salt Flat (Chile) until
        31 December 2030.

(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
        the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraphs 5 (d) and 9 of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
        certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
        adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
