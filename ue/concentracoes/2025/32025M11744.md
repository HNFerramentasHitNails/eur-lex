Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 17 January 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Lawrence
          J. Ellison and David Ellison (the “Ellison Family”, US) and RedBird Capital Partners
          Management LLC (“RedBird”, US) will acquire within the meaning of
          Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of National
          Amusements, Inc. (“NAI”, US). The Ellison Family will also acquire within the
          meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control of Paramount
          Global (“Paramount”, US). The two acquisitions are interdependent and therefore
          constitute a single concentration within the meaning of the Merger Regulation. The
          concentration is accomplished by way of purchase of shares. (3)

        of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.

Commission européenne/Europese Commissie, 1049 Bruxelles/Brussel, BELGIQUE/BELGIË – Tel. +32 22991111
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

        -    The Ellison Family invests in a wide portfolio of companies across various
             industries. Amongst others, the Ellison Family currently solely controls
             Skydance Media, LLC (“Skydance”), a media company that creates
             entertainment for global audiences. Skydance has studios in Los Angeles, Silicon
             Valley, Stamford, and Madrid,

        -    RedBird is a private investment firm headquartered in New York, which focuses
             on three core industry verticals, namely: Sports, Media & Entertainment, and
             Financial Services,

        -    NAI is a cinema operator and media holding company that operates 759 movie
             screens in the U.S., the UK, and Latin America,

        -    Paramount is a global media, streaming and entertainment company that creates
             content and experiences for audiences worldwide. Paramount’s portfolio of
             brands includes, among others: CBS, Paramount Pictures, Nickelodeon, MTV,
             Comedy Central, BET, Paramount+ and Pluto TV.

(3)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
        the notified concentration falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
        concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(4)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified concentration and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
        adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
        the EEA Agreement.
