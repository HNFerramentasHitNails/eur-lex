Dear Sir or Madam,

(1)       On 3 February 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Allianz
          Direct Versicherungs-AG (“AZ Direct”), (Germany), controlled by Allianz SE
          (Germany), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger
          Regulation sole control over the whole of iptiQ EMEA P&C S.A. (“iptiQ”),
          (Luxembourg), controlled by Swiss Re Ltd., (Switzerland), by way of purchase of
          shares. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    AZ Direct is an insurance company active in non-life insurances and insurance
               broker,
          –    iptiQ is an insurance company providing non-life and non-health insurance.

(3)       After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
          the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
          paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
          concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
