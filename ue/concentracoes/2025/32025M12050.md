       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which KBC
       Bank NV (‘KBC’, Belgium), controlled by KBC Group N.V. (‘KBC Group’,
       Belgium) will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1) (b) of the Merger
       Regulation sole control of the whole 365.bank, a.s. (‘365.bank’, Slovakia),
       controlled by J&T Finance Group SE (‘J&T’, Czechia) by way of purchase of
       shares. (3)
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      The KBC group is an integrated bank-insurance group, catering mainly for
              retail, private banking, SME and corporate clients. Core markets of the KBC
              group are Belgium, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary and Bulgaria,
       –      365.bank is a commercial bank in Slovakia with a full range of products and
              a particularly strong focus on retail customers. 365.bank operates as a full-
              service bank offering a wide range of services and products to individuals
              and corporate clients.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
