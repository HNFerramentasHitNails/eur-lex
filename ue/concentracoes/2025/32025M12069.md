Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 23 July 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which the
       undertakings Bain Capital Credit L.P. (‘Bain Capital Credit’, United States),
       controlled by Bain Capital, LP (‘Bain Capital’, United States), and Intermediate
       Capital Group plc (‘ICG’, United Kingdom) will acquire within the meaning of
       Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of the
       undertaking SG Global Midco Limited (‘Study Group’, United Kingdom),
       controlled by Ardian (France) by way of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      Bain Capital Credit is a global credit specialist and is active across Europe,
              Asia Pacific and the US,

      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2025/4349, 31.7.2025.
       –      ICG is a global alternative asset manager active in structured and private
              equity, private debt, real assets, and credit, across Europe, Asia Pacific and
              the US,
       –      Study Group provides educational services aimed at preparing international
              students for higher education, across Europe, the Americas, Africa and Asia
              Pacific.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
