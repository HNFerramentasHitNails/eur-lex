Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 11 March 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
        concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Paloma
        Rheem Holdings Co., Ltd (‘Paloma Rheem’, Japan), controlled by KMT Asset
        Management, Ltd (Japan) intends to acquire, within the meaning of
        Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation, sole control over the whole of the
        undertaking Fujitsu General Limited (‘Fujitsu General’, Japan), controlled by
        Fujitsu Limited (Japan). The concentration is to be accomplished by way of
        purchase of shares, following a public bid announced on 6 January 2025.(3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      Paloma Rheem is a holding company of Paloma Co., Ltd., which operates
               in the gas water heater and gas stove fields in Japan, and Rheem
               Manufacturing Company, which operates in the water heater and HVAC
               (heating, ventilation and air conditioning) fields mainly in North and South
               America, Europe, and Australia,
        –      Fujitsu General manufactures and supplies air conditioning equipment and
               systems for residential, commercial and industrial applications. It is also
               active in the manufacturing of information and communication systems
               (which includes firefighting and disaster prevention systems), and
