Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 12 September 2025, the European Commission received notification of a
        proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, and
        following a referral pursuant to Article 4(5) of the Merger Regulation, by which
        Platinum Equity Group (United States) will acquire within the meaning of Article
        3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control over the whole of the undertaking
        NCH Corporation (‘NCH’, United States), currently controlled by Ranger
        Holding LLC, (United States) by way of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      Platinum Equity Group is a private equity investment firm specialised in the
               merger, acquisition and operation of companies in a broad range of
               businesses. One of its portfolio companies is Solenis (United States), a
               provider of (i) water treatment solutions, in particular for industrial water
               treatment chemicals and services, (ii) process chemicals, (iii) pulp and paper
               coatings, additives and other chemicals, and (iv) related monitoring and
               control services and digital solutions,

        –      NCH is a provider of industrial, commercial, and institutional maintenance
               products and services. With relevance to the proposed transaction, NCH is

(3)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
        concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(4)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
        is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
        Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
