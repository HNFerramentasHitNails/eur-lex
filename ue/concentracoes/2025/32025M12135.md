Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 30 October 2025, the European Commission received notification of a
       proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
       Waters Corporation (“Waters”, United States) will acquire within the meaning of
       Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control over certain subsidiaries and
       assets of the Biosciences and Diagnostic Solutions business units (“Target”) of
       Becton, Dickinson and Company (“BD”, United States) by way of purchase of
       shares. (3)
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      Waters is a specialty measurement company that develops analytical
              workflow solutions, primarily involving liquid chromatography, mass
              spectrometry and thermal analysis innovations serving the life, materials
              and food sciences industries,
       –      The Target comprises BD’s Biosciences and Diagnostic Solutions business
              units.
              –      BD Biosciences is a provider of immunology and cancer research
                     solutions and related clinical diagnostics, including flow cytometry

       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
