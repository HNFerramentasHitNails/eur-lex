Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 21 August 2025, the European Commission received notification of a
        proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
        Tata AutoComp Systems Limited (‘Tata AutoComp’, India), ultimately
        controlled by Tata Sons (India), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b)
        of the Merger Regulation sole control of the assets of International Automotive
        Components Group Sweden AB (‘Assets of International Automotive
        Components Group’, Sweden), by way of purchase of assets. (3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –     Tata AutoComp is active in the manufacture and supply of a wide range of
              automotive components. Tata Sons is an investment holding company that
              generates revenues in the EEA across multiple sectors, including IT and
              engineering consultancy, aviation, automotive components, connectivity,
              and manufacturing activities,
        –     The Assets of International Automotive Components Group, which are
              currently held by the bankruptcy estate managed by an administrator
              appointed by the District Court of Gothenburg following International
              Automotive Components Group Sweden AB’s bankruptcy filing,

      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2025/4853, 29.8.2025.
              manufacture and supply components to the car manufacturing industry,
              mainly to Swedish customers.
(3)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(4)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
