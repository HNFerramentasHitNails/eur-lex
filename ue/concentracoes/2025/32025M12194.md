Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 25 November 2025, the European Commission received notification of a
       proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
       Integrity Lux Bidco S.à r.l. (Luxembourg), controlled by KKR & Co. Inc. (USA)
       (together, “KKR”) and SSCP Build Holdings SCA (Luxembourg), controlled by
       Stirling Square Capital Partners Fifth Fund (Jersey, “SSCP Fifth Fund”) (together,
       “SSCP”) will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger
       Regulation joint control over the whole of the undertaking SSCP Build HoldCo
       AB (Sweden, “Infobric”), controlled by SSCP Fifth Fund by way of purchase of
       shares.(3)




(1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2025 /6557, 8.12.2025.
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      KKR is a global investment firm that offers alternative asset management
              as well as capital markets and insurance solutions.
       –      SSCP is an investment fund that seeks to make, hold, monitor and realise
              investments primarily via management buy-ins, growth capital and other
              private equity transactions through Europe. SSCP Fifth Fund is one of four
              funds managed by the SSCP investment structure.
(3)    Infobric is a construction software provider to the construction industry in Europe,
       focused on the build phase of the construction.
(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
