(14)      The first potential segmentation is between the operation of on-street and off-

          as garages and lots, whether indoors or outdoors. (5) By contrast, on-street parking
          refers to the supply of parking spaces for vehicles to park on the street, e.g. along
          the sidewalk or any place that is not an identified parking lot or car park. (6)
(15)      In a previous decision, the Commission considered that off-street and on-street
          paid public parking services could differ (i) from the user’s perspective since off-
          street services would be considered more secured and more expensive, and (ii)
          from the parking operators’ perspective, since on-street and off-street parking
          services would require different kinds of service provisions. (7) However, the
          Commission ultimately left the market definition open. (8)
(16)      For the purpose of the present decision, the Commission considers that the
          question of whether a segmentation should be made between on-street and off-
          street paid public services can be left open. As explained below, the Transaction
          does not raise serious doubts as to its compatibility with the internal market or
          any substantial part of it irrespective of the market definition.
