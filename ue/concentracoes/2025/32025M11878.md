          concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Boliden
          Mineral AB (‘Boliden’, Sweden), controlled by Boliden AB (Sweden) will acquire
          within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation control of the whole
          of Zinkgruvan Mining AB (‘Zinkgruvan’, Sweden), Somincor-Sociedade Mineira de
          Neves-Corvo S.A (‘Somincor’, Portugal), and North Atlantic Natural Resources AB
          (‘NAN’, Sweden), all currently controlled by Lundin Mining Corporation (Sweden).
          The concentration is accomplished by way of purchase of shares. (3)

(2)       The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
          –    Boliden is active in exploration, mining, smelting and refining of certain non-
               ferrous metals as well as the supply of metal concentrates, metals and certain
               by-products,
          –    Zinkgruvan owns the Zinkgruvan mine located in South-Central Sweden, which
               includes a processing plant for the production of non-ferrous metal concentrates
               (i.e., copper, zinc and lead concentrates),
          –    Somincor owns the Neves-Corvo mine concession, a mineral deposit located in
               the Alentejo district of southern Portugal, which includes two processing plants
               for the production of non-ferrous metal concentrates (i.e., copper, zinc and lead
               concentrates),
