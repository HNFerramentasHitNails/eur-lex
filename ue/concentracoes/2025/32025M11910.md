Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 2 May 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
        concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Mapfre
        Asistencia, Compañia Internacional de Seguros y Reaseguros S.A. (“MAPFRE”,
        Spain), controlled by Fundación MAPFRE (Spain), and Vittoria Assicurazioni
        S.p.A. (“VAS”, Italy), controlled by Yafa S.p.A. (Italy), which is in turn subject
        to the ultimate direct control of a natural person, will acquire within the meaning
        of Articles 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of MAWDY
        Services S.p.A (“MAWDY Services”) and Vittoria Servizi Legali S.r.l. (“VSL”)




( 1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
       on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
       replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
       terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
( 2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
        (together, the “JVs”). The concentration is accomplished by way of purchase of
        shares. (3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –     MAPFRE is a Spanish company active in the insurance and reinsurance
              sectors worldwide. It is ultimately controlled by Fundación MAPFRE, which
              carries out non-profit activities of general interest in areas such as culture,
              health promotion, prevention, road safety, insurance, and social protection,
        –     VAS is an Italian company active in the insurance and reinsurance sectors.
(3)     The business activities of the JVs are the following:
        -    MAWDY Services is an Italian company currently controlled by MAPFRE,
             and is mainly active in insurance distribution and as a provider of assistance
             services in various sectors. MAWDY Services also provides outsourced
             management solutions for the automotive and insurance sectors,
        -    VSL is an Italian company currently indirectly controlled by VAS. VSL is
             active in the provision of digital services, including in the healthcare sector
             (through ComeStai S.p.A) and in the legal sector (through EasyLegal S.r.l.).
(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraphs 5(b) and 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
        certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
        is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
        Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
