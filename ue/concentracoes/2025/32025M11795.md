(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –     Yunex, ultimately controlled by Edizione, develops innovative mobility
             ecosystems and services for the smart city,
       –     VVP is a full function joint venture between Brisa and Ascendi which operates

             Blankenburg toll route in the Netherlands,
       –     Ascendi, ultimately controlled by Ardian, exploits toll roads in Portugal and
             additionally is active with road operation and maintenance, intelligent transport
             systems and ancillary maintenance management. Ascendi has no current
             activities in the Netherlands.

(3)    The business activities of Triangle JV will be the provision of services in relation to
       an awarded tender for the introduction of a truck levy on all highways and certain
       regional motorways in the Netherlands. In particular, Triangle JV will provide on
       board equipment to truck holders/owners and ensure proper registration of truck
       movement and collection of levies. Triangle JV will be responsible for payment of
       levies to the toll collector (Road Transport Agency, acting on behalf of the Minister
       of Infrastructure and Water Management) and ensure all related customer support by
       providing a dedicated back office and customer contact center. Triangle JV will not
       be active in the construction of motorways and road infrastructure.

(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraphs 5 (b) and (d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
       certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)

(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
       adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
