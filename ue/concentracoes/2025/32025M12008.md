        Norway), and Strawberry Club AS (‘Strawberry Club’, Norway), a subsidiary of

        of Articles 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of Spenn Group

        AS (‘Spenn’, Norway), presently jointly controlled by Norwegian Reward and
        Strawberry Club, by way of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      Reitan Retail holds operations in the grocery, convenience, mobility fuel
               and energy, and car wash sectors across the Nordic and Baltic countries,
        –      Norwegian Reward operates a customer loyalty programme for
               Norwegian’s airline customers,
        –      Strawberry Club operates a customer loyalty programme for Strawberry’s
               hotel customers.
(3)     Spenn operates a multi-partner platform that facilitates the collection and
        redemption of loyalty points in the Nordic countries.
(4)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(b) and (c) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
        certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(5)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
        is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
        Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
