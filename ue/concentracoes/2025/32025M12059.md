Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)     On 11 September 2025, the European Commission received notification of a
        proposed concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which
        the undertakings Pandox AB (‘Pandox’, Sweden) and Scandic Hotels Holding AB
        (‘Scandic’, Sweden) controlled by Scandic Hotels Group AB (Sweden) will
        acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation joint
        control over the Operating Business of the undertaking Dalata Hotel Group plc.
        (‘Dalata’, Ireland) and Pandox will acquire sole control of Dalata’s Property
        Business. The concentration is accomplished by way of public bid announced on
        15 July 2025 and by way of contract.(3)
(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
        –      Pandox is a hotel property company present mainly in Northern Europe,

        –      Dalata is a hotel owner and operator mainly active in the UK and Ireland.
               Dalata’s Property Business comprises the owned and freehold or long
               leasehold properties of Dalata. The Operating Business comprises the assets
               and employees to run the hotel operation business of Dalata.
(3)     After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
        that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
        paragraph 5(d) and 8(a) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
        certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(4)     For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
        Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
        compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
        is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
        Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
