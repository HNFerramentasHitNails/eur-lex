        concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which the
        undertakings Ingredion Germany GmbH (‘Ingredion’, Germany) and AGRANA
        Stärke GmbH (‘AGRANA’, Austria) intend to acquire within the meaning of Articles
        3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control of the undertaking AGFD
        Țăndărei s.r.l. (‘AGFD’, Romania), currently a wholly owned and solely controlled
        subsidiary of AGRANA, by way of purchase of shares.3

(2)     The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:

        – Ingredion is a global provider of ingredients solutions that produces starches,
          sweeteners, and other ingredients from renewable raw materials such as maize,
          tapioca, the stevia plant, and others,

        – AGRANA is a global processor of agricultural raw materials, structured in three
          operational divisions: sugar, starch, and fruit.

(3)    The business activities of the undertaking AGFD are the following: the operation of
       a maize starch production facility in Romania that currently manufactures starches
       from regular and high amylose maize as well as non-starch products; following an
       upgrade of the production facility, also the production of more advanced starch
       products, in particular modified waxy and high amylose maize starch products, all to
       be sold in Europe.

(4)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded that
       the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraphs 5 (b) and 5 (d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for
       certain concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004.4

(5)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision is
       adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and Article 57 of
