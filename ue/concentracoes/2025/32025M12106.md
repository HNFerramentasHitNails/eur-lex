           Oaktree Capital Holdings, LLC (‘Oaktree’, United States), jointly controlled by
           Oaktree Capital Group Holdings GP, LLC (‘OCGH GP’, United States) and
           Brookfield Corporation (‘Brookfield’, United States), will acquire within the
           meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation sole control over the whole
           of the undertaking Superior Industries International, Inc. (‘SUP’, United States)
           by way of purchase of shares. (3)
(2)        The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
           –     Oaktree is a global alternative investment management firm that invests in
                 distressed debt, high yield bonds, convertible securities, senior loans,
                 corporate control, real estate, emerging market equities and mezzanine
                 finance,
           –     SUP designs and manufactures aluminium wheels for sale to Original
                 Equipment Manufacturers in North America and Europe, and to the
                 aftermarket in Europe.
(3)        After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
           that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
