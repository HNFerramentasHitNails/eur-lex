           Commission decision pursuant to Article 6(1)(b) of Council Regulation

           Economic Area (2)



Dear Sir or Madam,


(1)    On 26 June 2025, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
       concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation, by which Achmea
       B.V. (“Achmea”, the Netherlands), ultimately controlled by Vereniging Achmea,
       and ELG Holding Limited (“ELG Holding”, Cayman Islands), a subsidiary of
       investment funds managed by affiliates of Sixth Street Partners, LLC (USA)
       (together, “Sixth Street”), will acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and
       3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over Achmea’s wholly owned
       subsidiary Achmea Pensioen- en Levensverzekeringen N.V (“AP&L”) and ELG
       Holding’s wholly owned subsidiary Lifetri Groep B.V. (“Lifetri”), both
       established in the Netherlands, by way of purchase of shares. (3)



(1)   OJ L 24, 29.1.2004, p. 1 (the ‘Merger Regulation’). With effect from 1 December 2009, the Treaty
      on the Functioning of the European Union (‘TFEU’) has introduced certain changes, such as the
      replacement of ‘Community’ by ‘Union’ and ‘common market’ by ‘internal market’. The
      terminology of the TFEU will be used throughout this decision.
(2)   OJ L 1, 3.1.1994, p. 3 (the ‘EEA Agreement’).
(3)   OJ C, C/2025/3701, 8.7.2025.
(2)    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are the following:
       –      Achmea is a Dutch company operating in the field of financial services. It
              offers a wide range of insurance, banking and mortgage products and related
              services,
       –      ELG Holding is a subsidiary of investment funds managed by affiliates of
              Sixth Street. Sixth Street is a global investment firm.
(3)    AP&L encompasses Achmea’s pension and life insurance business in the
       Netherlands. AP&L has an active offering of term life insurance products and
       variable annuities, as well as a closed book portfolio of collective pension
       products and individual life insurance products.
(4)    Lifetri is a Dutch insurance company that focuses on the servicing of closed books
       of collective defined benefit pension products and individual life insurance
       products.
(5)    After examination of the notification, the European Commission has concluded
       that the notified operation falls within the scope of the Merger Regulation and of
       paragraph 5(d) of the Commission Notice on a simplified treatment for certain
       concentrations under Council Regulation (EC) No 139/2004. (4)
(6)    For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified treatment, the European
       Commission has decided not to oppose the notified operation and to declare it
       compatible with the internal market and with the EEA Agreement. This decision
       is adopted in application of Article 6(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation and
       Article 57 of the EEA Agreement.
