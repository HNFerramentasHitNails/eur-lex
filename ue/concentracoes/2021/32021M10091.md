Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 22 December 2020, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which TowerBrook
      Capital Partners L.P. (“TowerBrook”, USA) and Warburg Pincus LLC (“Warburg
      Pincus”, USA) acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) of the Merger Regulation
      joint control over the whole of AA Plc (“AA”, UK) by way of public bid announced
      on 25 November 2020.4

2.    The business activities of the undertakings concerned are:

          TowerBrook is an investment management firm and focuses on making
           investments in large and middle market European and North American
           companies.

          Warburg Pincus is a private equity firm headquartered in New York, United
           States.
