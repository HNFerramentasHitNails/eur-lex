      Ltd (“Mitsui”, Japan) and Veolia Japan K.K. (“Veolia”, Japan), controlled by Veolia
      Environnement (France), acquire within the meaning of Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the
      Merger Regulation joint control of a newly created company constituting a joint
      venture, namely Circular PET Co., Ltd (“Circular PET”, Japan). The concentration is
      accomplished by way of purchase of shares.4
