Dear Sir or Madam,

1.    On 14 April 2021, the European Commission received notification of a proposed
      concentration pursuant to Article 4 of the Merger Regulation by which Clearlake
      Capital Group L.P. (United States), TA Associates Management, L.P. (United States)
      and Charlesbank Capital Partners, LLP (United States) acquire within the meaning of
      Article 3(1)(b) and 3(4) of the Merger Regulation joint control over the whole of
      Ivanti Software, Inc. (United States) currently controlled by Clearlake and TA
      Associates, by way of purchase of shares.3

4.   For the reasons set out in the Notice on a simplified procedure, the European
