# 31975Y1215(02)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Publication of the updated version of the Annex to Council Regulation (EEC) No 2603/69 of 20 December 1969 establishing common rules for exports

The Annex to Regulation (EEC) No 2603/69 (1), as amended by Regulations (EEC) No 234/71 (2), (EEC) No 2182/71 (3) and (EEC) No 1275/75 (4), is published hereinafter in its present version.

This communication is published solely for information, only the texts of the abovementioned Regulations being authentic.

(1) OJ No L 324, 27.12.1969, p. 25. (2) OJ No L 28, 4.2.1971, p. 2. (3) OJ No L 231, 14.10.1971, p. 4. (4) OJ No L 131, 22.5.1975, p. 1.
