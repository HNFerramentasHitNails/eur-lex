# 31975Y0809(01)

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Preâmbulo

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES,

HAS ADOPTED THIS RESOLUTION:

## Texto

The Council asks the Commission to propose to it no later than when it submits its price proposals for the 1976/77 marketing year the measures appropriate to dealing with all the problems raised by the development of varieties of wheat unsuitable for making bread, with due regard for the practicalities of management and supervision.
