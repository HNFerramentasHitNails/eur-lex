## Texto

Reports on the results of market research measures within the Community and measures concerning the expansion of markets for Community milk products outside the Community

Pursuant to Article 4 of Council Regulation (EEC) No 1079/77 on the coresponsibility levy in the milk sector (1) the Commission, having regard to Regulations (EEC) No 723/78 (2) and (EEC) No 1024/78 (3), granted aid in respect of research projects for the expansion of markets for Community milk products within and outside the Community. All contractors were required to submit a report on the results of their research work.

In order to make these results available to as wide a circle of interested persons as possible, a central office was designated in each Member State, which has forwarded the reports to the Commission or will shortly be doing so. Interested persons can go to the central offices and there consult the reports and have copies made at their own expense.

The central offices and the titles of the research work undertaken are listed below.
