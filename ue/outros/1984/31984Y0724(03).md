# 31984Y0724(03)

## Texto

Modification to the rules of procedure of the Regional Policy Committee (84/C 195/03)

In its meeting of 5 June 1984, the Regional Policy Committee modified its rules of procedure (1) ; to take account of the entry of Greece, the majorities, mentioned in paragraphs 2 and 3 of Article 3, are increased from eleven to twelve votes. (1) OJ No L 320, 11.12.1975, p. 17.
