# Directiva do Conselho, de 14 de Junho de 1966, relativa à comercialização de sementes de plantas forrageiras

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Council Directive of 14 June 1966 on the marketing of fodder plant seed

Council Directive No 66/401/EEC of 14 June 1966 on the marketing of fodder plant seed ( OJ No 125 , 11 . 7 . 1966 , p. 2298/66 ) and the amendments arising out of the Acts set out below :

1 . Directive No 69/63/EEC ( OJ No L 48 , 26 . 2 . 1969 , p. 8 ) ;

2 . Directive No 71/162/EEC ( OJ No L 87 , 17 . 4 . 1971 , p. 24 ) ;

3 . Directive No 72/274/EEC ( OJ No L 171 , 29 . 7 . 1972 , p. 37 ) ;

4 . Directive No 72/418/EEC ( OJ No L 287 , 26 . 12 . 1972 , p. 22 ) ;

5 . Acts of Accession ( OJ , Special Edition , 27 . 3 . 1972 , p. 14 ) and the Council Decision of 1 January 1973 adjusting the documents concerning the Accession of new Member States to the European Communities ( OJ No L 2 , 1 . 1 . 1973 , p. 1 ) ;

6 . Directive No 73/438/EEC ( OJ No L 356 , 27 . 12 . 1973 , p. 79 ) ;

are hereby coordinated .

This coordination is without legal status . Hence , the preamble has been omitted .

The numbers in brackets at certain articles correspond to the above numbering and refer to the last amendment of the basic Act .
