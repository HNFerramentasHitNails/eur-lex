## Texto

COUNCIL RESOLUTION of 20 November 1974 on the management of quantitative cereal supplies intended for the Community

THE COUNCIL OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES,

desiring to achieve improved management of quantitative supplies of cereal imports into the Community, in particular maize, and to facilitate information on current and future imports;

AGREES,

on the need to improve the availability of statistical data by closer collaboration between the Commission and the Member States.
