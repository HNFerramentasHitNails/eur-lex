# Directiva do Conselho, de 29 de Setembro de 1970, que diz respeito ao catalogo comum das variedades das espécies de plantas agrícolas

**Texto:** versão original publicada no Jornal Oficial (sem alterações posteriores, se as houver).

**Língua:** o Cellar não tem este acto em português; o texto abaixo está em **inglês**.

## Texto

Council Directive of 29 September 1970 on the common catalogue of varieties of agricultural plant species

Council Directive No 70/457/EEC of 29 September 1970 on the common catalogue of varieties of agricultural plant species ( OJ No L 225 , 12 . 10 . 1970 , p. 1 ) and the amendments arising out of the acts set out below :

1 . Directive No 72/274/EEC ( OJ No L 171 , 29 . 7 . 1972 , p. 37 ) ;

2 . Directive No 72/418/EEC ( OJ No L 287 , 26 . 12 . 1972 , p. 22 ) ;

3 . Act of Accession ( OJ , Special Edition , 27 . 3 . 1972 , p. 14 ) and the Council Decision of 1 January 1973 adjusting the documents concerning the Accession of new Member States to the European Communities ( OJ No L 2 , 1 . 1 . 1973 , p. 1 ) ;

4 . Directive No 73/438/EEC ( OJ No L 356 , 27 . 12 . 1973 , p. 79 ) ;

are hereby coordinated .

This coordination is without legal status . Hence , the preamble has been omitted .

The numbers in brackets at certain Articles correspond to the above numbering and refer to the last amendment of the basic Act .
