Referred to collectively as the "Parties" or individually as a "Party",



Recalling that the European Central Bank has the exclusive right to authorise the issue of banknotes
within those Member States of the European Union whose currency is the euro and that this exclusive
right includes the responsibility for the protection of the euro against counterfeiting,



Acknowledging that the European Central Bank has an active role in the prevention and detection of the
counterfeiting of the euro; that Guideline ECB/1999/3 on certain provisions regarding euro banknotes, as
amended on 26 August 1999 1 , established the Counterfeit Analysis Centre ("CAC") within the European
Central Bank, in order to centralise the technical analysis of counterfeit euro banknotes and data relating
to the counterfeiting of the euro; and that Council Regulation (EC) No 1338/2001 of 28 June 2001 laying
down measures necessary for the protection of the euro against counterfeiting 2· in particular Article 3
thereof, provides that the European Central Bank shall gather and store technical and statistical data
relating to counterfeit euro banknotes discovered in non-member countries,

national legislation applicable to it 3 to investigate and analyse all counterfeit banknotes seized or
recovered by the banks and the police irrespective of whether they are of domestic or foreign currency,
and to store all such counterfeits, and



Sharing a joint determination to combat the threats arising from the counterfeiting of the euro in general,
and wanting to co-ordinate their efforts within the framework of the missions assigned to them, and to
cooperate. within their respective competencies, in particular in the field of detection of counterfeit euro
banknotes,



HAVE AGREED AS FOLLOWS:



                                                       Article 1

                                                      Purpose

1.    The purpose of this Agreement is to establish a framework for cooperation between the Parties,
      within their respective competences and subject to their respective rules and regulations. thus
      facilitating the prevention and detection of counterfeiting of euro banknotes in the Republic of North
      Macedonia.

2.    This Agreement is without prejudice to the rights and obligations established in the agreements and
      other arrangements between the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia and Interpol,
      as well as to those established in the Agreements and other arrangements between the European
      Central Bank and Interpol or Europol respectively.

                                            Exchange of information

Subject to the Parties' internal rules and regulations and to such arrangements as may be necessary for
the safeguarding of confidential information, exchange of information and documents concerning matters
of common interest as indicated in Article 1 ( 1 ), shall take place between the Parties for the purpose of
and in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement, and shall not include personal data.

       the procedure for detection and withdrawal of counterfeit money (published in the Official Gazette No.

                                                  Article 3

                               Obligations of the European Central Bank
1.   The European Central Bank shall provide the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia
     with information regarding the technical specifications and technical features of legal tender euro
     banknotes upon the request of the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia.

2.   The European Central Bank shall provide the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia
     with sufficient technical data relating to counterfeit euro banknotes that have been classified as
     common classes so as to enable the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia to identify
     them.

3.   To the extent possible the European Central Bank shall stand ready to consult with the National
     Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia in order to facilitate the proper implementation of this
     Agreement.



                                                  Article 4

                Obligations of the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia
1.   Whenever the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia receives information concerning a
     new type of euro banknote counterfeit, which does not yet correspond to any of the common
     classes of which the European Central Bank has informed it, the National Bank of the Republic of
     North Macedonia shall promptly send samples of any such new type of euro banknote counterfeit
     received by it or otherwise in its possession to the European Central Bank. The number of samples
     required shall be agreed on a case by case basis by the Parties.

2.   The samples shall be accompanied by a completed form "Reported Euro Counterfeits", which shall
     include, if available, the following information:

     (a)     the total number of the counterfeits that have been seized or recovered;

     (b)     the date and location of the seizures or recoveries; and,

     (c)     an assessment of whether or not the source of the counterfeits has been identified by the
             competent authority.

     The template form "Reported Euro Counterfeits" is laid down in the Annex.

     If for any reason it is not possible for the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia to send
     the European Central Bank any of the samples referred to in paragraph 1, it shall instead send
     scanned images of such samples as laid down in the Annex, together with a completed form
     "Reported Euro Counterfeits".

3.   In addition to submitting either a sample, or scanned images of every new type of suspected
     counterfeit euro banknotes to the European Central Bank the National Bank of the Republic of
     North Macedonia shall, on a monthly basis, send to the European Central Bank statistical reports
                                                         4
      on all euro counterfeits discovered in a particular month in the Republic of North Macedonia. The
      European Central bank shall provide the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia with a
      template for this statistical information.

4.    Within 6 months after the date when this Agreement enters into force the National Bank of the
      Republic of North Macedonia shall provide the European Central Bank with a statistical overview of
      all counterfeit euro banknotes that have been found in Republic of North Macedonia prior to this
      date. The template mentioned in paragraph 3 of this Article shall be used for this report.

5.    The National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia shall take all necessary and feasible
      measures, according to its national legal system, to ensure that there is no breach of the copyright
      of the euro banknote designs held by the European Central Bank.



                                                      Article 5

                                                   Major incidents

The European Central Bank and the National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia shall inform each
other immediately of major counterfeit incidents concerning euro banknotes.



                                                      Article 6

                                Means of communication between Parties

All information exchanged between the European Central Bank and the National Bank of the Republic of
North Macedonia shall be exclusively communicated by means of secure communication. The Parties
shall agree the means of secure communication separately. All files sent electronically shall be encrypted.



                                                      Article 7

                                               Contact persons

The Parties shall designate the contact persons for the purposes of this Agreement separately by an
exchange of letters between the President of the European Central Bank and the Governor of the
National Bank of the Republic of North Macedonia.



                                                      Article 8

                                         Security and confidentiality

1.    The Parties shall ensure that all information received on the basis of this Agreement, as well as the
      processing of such information, is always subject to security and confidentiality standards, which
      are at least equivalent to the standards applied by the originating Party to such information. All
      such information shall receive a level of protection, which is at least equivalent to the level of
      protection offered by the measures applied to that information by the originating Party.

                                                      ANNEX
             TRANSMISSION OF IMAGES OF COUNTERFEIT EURO BANKNOTES


     For each case of counterfeit euro banknotes sent electronically, there should be:

     1.    The entire front and reverse of the banknote, shown flat and undistorted, scanned as a 24-bitmap
           image at a resolution 100 dots per inch (dpi) for the screen image or 400 dots per inch (dpi) for the
           printed image.

     2.    Any regions of special interest (e.g. microtext) should also be scanned as a 24-bitmap image at a
           resolution of 100 dots per inch (dpi) for the screen image or 400 dots per inch (dpi) for the printed
           image.
