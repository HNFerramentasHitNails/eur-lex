## Texto

Declarations of the new Member States provided for in Article 5 of Regulation (EEC) No 1408/71 of the Council, of 14 June 1971, on the application of social security schemes to employed persons and their families moving within the Community (1)

A. KINGDOM OF DENMARK

I. Legislation and schemes referred to in Article 4 (1) and (2) of the Regulation

1. DENMARK WITH THE EXCEPTION OF GREENLAND

(a) Sickness and maternity benefits: - Law No 311 of 9 June 1971 on the public health service (lov om offentlig sygesikring), as subsequently amended,

- Law No 260 of 7 June 1972 on daily allowances in cases of sickness and childbirth (lov om dagpenge ved sygdom og fødsel), as subsequently amended,

- Law No 237 of 14 June 1969 on hospital services (lov om sygehusvassen), as subsequently amended,

- Law No 282 of 7 June 1972 on pregnancy hygiene and aid in childbirth (lov om svangerskabshygiejne og fødselshjaelp), as subsequently amended;

(b) Invalidity benefits, including those intended for the maintenance or improvement of earning capacity: - Law No 156 of 15 April 1970 on validity pensions (lovbekendtgørelse om invalidepension), as subsequently amended,

- Law No 170 of 29 April 1960 on rehabilitation (lov om revalidering), as subsequently amended but excepting Article 7;

(c) Old-age benefits: - Law No 155 of 15 April 1970 on old-age pensions (lovbekendtgørelse om folkepension), as subsequently amended, (1)OJ No L 149, 5.7.1971, p. 2.

Law No 251 of 6 June 1967 on supplementary pensions for employed persons (lovbekendtgørelse om arbejdsmarkedets tillaegspension), as subsequently amended;

(d) Survivors' benefits: - Law No 157 of 15 April 1970 on pensions and aid to widows etc (lovbekendtgørelse om pension og hjaelp til enker m.fl.), as subsequently amended, but excepting Chapter II;

(e) Benefits in respect of accidents at work and occupational diseases: - Law No 137 of 26 April 1968 on accident insurance (lovbekendtgørelse om ulykkesforsikring), as subsequently amended;

(f) Death grants: - Law No 311 of 9 June 1971 on the public health service, as subsequently amended,

- Law No 137 of 26 April 1968 on accident insurance, as subsequently amended;

(g) Unemployment benefits: - Law (lovbekendtgørelse) No 314 of 22 June 1971, as subsequently amended,

(h) Family benefits: - Law No 388 of 8 July 1969 on family allowances and other family benefits (lovbekendtgørelse om børnetilskud og andre familieydelser), as subsequently amended.

2. GREENLAND

(a) Sickness and maternity benefits: - Act of 29 October 1960 on charges for hospitalisation, medical care etc, for the Greenland Health Service (bekendtgørelse om takster for sygehusophold, laegebehandling m.v. for sundhedsvaesenet i Grønland), as subsequently amended;

(c) Old-age benefits: - Regulation of the Regional Council of 2 October 1967, ratified on 21 December 1967, on old-age pensions in Greenland (landsradsvedtaegt om aldersrente i Grønland), as subsequently amended;

(e) Benefits in respect of accidents at work and occupational diseases: - Regulation for Greenland of 2 February 1962 on insurance against the after-effects of accidents (anordning for Grønland om forsikring mod følger af ulykkestilfaelde), as subsequently amended;

(f) Death grants: - Regulation for Greenland of 2 February 1962 on insurance against the after-effects of accidents, as subsequently amended;

(h) Family benefits: - Law No 49 of 25 February 1964 on family allowances in Greenland, as subsequently amended.

1. DENMARK WITH THE EXCEPTION OF GREENLAND

None 2. GREENLAND

None III. Benefits referred to in Article 77 of the Regulation

1. DENMARK WITH THE EXCEPTION OF GREENLAND - Law No 388 of 8 July 1969 on family allowances and other family benefits, as subsequently amended;

B. IRELAND

1. Legislation and schemes referred to in Article 4 (1) and (2) of the Regulation

(a) Sickness and Maternity Benefits:

the provisions of the Social Welfare Acts 1952 to 1972 and of the regulations made thereunder, relating to Disability Benefit, Maternity Benefit and Treatment Benefit;

the provisions of the Health Acts 1947 to 1970 and of the regulations made thereunder.

(b) Invalidity Benefits:

the provisions of the Social Welfare Acts 1952 to 1972 and of the regulations made thereunder, relating to Invalidity Pension.

(c) Old-age Benefits:

the provisions of the Social Welfare Acts 1952 to 1972 and of the regulations made thereunder, relating to Old-Age (Contributory) Pension and Retirement Pension.

(d) Survivors' Benefits:

the provisions of the Social Welfare Acts 1952 to 1972 and of the Regulations made thereunder, relating to Widow's (Contributory) Pension and Orphan's (Contributory) Allowance.

(e) Benefits in respect of Accidents at Work and Occupational Diseases:

the provisions of the Social Welfare Acts 1952 to 1972 and of the regulations made thereunder, relating to Occupational Injuries Benefit.

the provisions of the Health Acts 1947 to 1970 and of the regulations made thereunder.

(f) Death Grants:

the provisions of the Social Welfare Acts 1952 to 1972 and of the regulations made thereunder, relating to Death Grants.

(g) Unemployment Benefits:

the provisions of the Social Welfare Acts 1952 to 1972 and of the regulations made thereunder, relating to Unemployment Benefit.

(h) Family benefits:

the provisions of the Social Welfare (Children's Allowances) Acts 1944 to 1970 and thereunder.

The Regulation shall apply to the above legislation from and including 1 April 1973.

2. Minimum benefits referred to in Article 50 of the Regulation:

the minimum Invalidity Pension, Retirement Pension, Old Age (Contributory) Pension and Widow's (Contributory) Pension provided for by the Social Welfare Acts 1952 to 1972 and by the regulations made thereunder.

3. Benefits referred to in Article 77 of the Regulation:

Children's Allowances under the Social Welfare (Children's Allowances) Acts 1944 to 1970 payable to persons in receipt of Retirement Pension, Old-Age (Contributory) Pension, Invalidity Pension, Disablement Pension, or pension by way of Death Benefit under the Social Welfare Acts 1952 to 1972, and increases of Retirement Pension, of Old-Age (Contributory) Pension and of Invalidity Pension in respect of qualified children under the Social Welfare Acts 1952 to 1972.

4. Benefits referred to in Article 78 of the Regulation:

Children's Allowances under the Social Welfare (Children's Allowances) Acts 1944 to 1970 payable in respect of orphans or in respect of children for whom increases of Widow's (Contributory) Pension are payable under the Social Welfare Acts 1952 to 1972, and Orphan's (Contributory) Allowance and increases of Widow's (Contributory) Pension payable in respect of children qualified under the latter Acts.

II. Minimum benefits referred to in Article 50 of the Regulation

The rates of flat-rate old-age pension and survivors' benefits depend upon the yearly average of contributions paid or credited throughout the insurance life and these rates are laid down in Regulations made under the legislation referred to at (1) and (7) (i) above.

(ii) GIBRALTAR

Family allowances ; child dependency allowances paid to pensioners ; guardian's allowance.
