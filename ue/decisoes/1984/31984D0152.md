## Preâmbulo

THE COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN

COMMUNITIES,

Having regard to the Treaty establishing the European Economic Community,

Having regard to Council Regulation (EEC) No 355/77 of 15 February 1977 on common measures to improve the conditions under which agricultural products are processed and marketed (1), as last amended by Regulation (EEC) No 3164/82 (2), and in particular Article 5 thereof,

Whereas on 1 September 1983 the Italian Government submitted the programmes for the processing and marketing of feed grains in the Regions of Tuscany, Marche, Umbria and Sardinia;

Whereas the said programmes concern the creation and modernization of equipment for drying and storage of feed grains with the aim of improving the utilization and marketing of these products; whereas they therefore constitute programmes within the meaning of Article 2 of Regulation (EEC) No 355/77;

Whereas the programmes contain the details referred to in Article 3 of Regulation (EEC) No 355/77, showing that the objectives laid down in Article 1 of that Regulation can be achieved in respect of this production; whereas the scheduled time for implementation of the programmes does not exceed the limit laid down in Article 3 (1) (g) of the Regulation;

Whereas the programmes can be approved only for applications foreseen under Article 24 (4) of Regulation (EEC) No 355/77;

Whereas the measures provided for in this Decision are in accordance with the opinion of the Standing Committee on Agricultural Structure,

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:

## Texto

### Article 1

1. The programmes for the processing and marketing of feed grains in the Regions of Tuscany, Marche, Umbria and Sardinia, pursuant to Regulation (EEC) No 355/77, submitted by the Italian Government on 1 September 1983 are hereby approved.

2. Approval of the programmes relates only to projects submitted before 30 April 1984.

### Article 2

This Decision is addressed to the Italian Republic.

Done at Brussels, 5 March 1984.

For the Commission

Poul DALSAGER

Member of the Commission
