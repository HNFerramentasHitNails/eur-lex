Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, and in particular Article 127(2)

and in particular Article 3.1, Article 22 and Article 34.1 thereof,

requirements for systemically important payment systems (ECB/2025/22) 1, and in particular Article 3(1)

Whereas:

(1)    The fourth indent of Article 127(2) of the Treaty and the fourth indent of Article 3.1 of the Statute of
       the European System of Central Banks and of the European Central Bank empower the Eurosystem
       to promote the smooth operation of payment systems.

(2)    The Eurosystem promotes the smooth operation of payment systems, inter alia, by conducting
       oversight.
(3)    To ensure the effective oversight of payment systems, the European Central Bank (ECB), through
       Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22), has implemented the principles for financial market
       infrastructures issued by the Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures (CPMI), previously
       known as the Committee on Payment and Settlement Systems (CPSS), and the Technical
       Committee of the International Organization of Securities Commission (IOSCO). Those principles
       harmonise and strengthen existing international oversight standards for, inter alia, systemically
       important payment systems. The provisions of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22) also
       reflect the experience and findings of the oversight assessments conducted on the basis of

HAS ADOPTED THIS DECISION:



                                                    Article 1

                                                  Definitions
Terms used in this Decision have the same meaning as those in Regulation (EU) 2025/1355
(ECB/2025/22).



                                                    Article 2

                                 Identification of SIPS and SIPS operator

1.     The Visa Europe Payment System shall be identified as a systemically important payment system
       for the purposes of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22) in accordance with Article 3(1), point
       (a), and the criteria under 3(1), point (b)(ii) and (iii), of that Regulation and on the basis of the Annex
       to this Decision.
2.     In its capacity as operator of the Visa Europe Payment System for the euro area, VISA EUROPE
       Limited, German Branch, in accordance with Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355
       (ECB/2025/22), shall ensure that this systemically important payment system complies with the
       requirements set out in Articles 8 to 27 and Article 29 of Regulation (EU) 2025/1355 (ECB/2025/22).



                                                    Article 3
                                             Competent authority

The European Central Bank (ECB) shall be the competent authority for the oversight of the Visa Europe
Payment System.
